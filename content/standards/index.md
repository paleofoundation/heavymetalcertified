<section id="method">

## What is published here

This register exposes the current government-anchored cells for governance review. The initial numeric limit is never selected from product occurrence. It is the strictest applicable sovereign maximum in the product's native basis, followed only when necessary by a disclosed sovereign read-across or an explicit nonnumeric control. Final certification use begins after governance approval of the row.

The canonical laboratory contract always includes eight totals: Pb, Cd, total arsenic, total mercury, Ni, Sn, Al, and total Cr. Inorganic arsenic and Cr(VI) are additional species decisions; methylmercury is conditional.
