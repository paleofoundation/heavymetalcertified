  <p style="font-size:13.5px;line-height:1.7;color:#736b6b;margin:0 0 20px;max-width:84ch">15 locked rows across 8 categories. Each publishes in the native basis of its own product form, and each has its own page. Numbering restarts per category, so a citation names both &mdash; <span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:12.5px;color:#9f2727">Seafood 3.1</span> and <span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:12.5px;color:#9f2727">Culinary Oils and Fats 3.1</span> are different standards.</p>
  <div style="border:1px dotted #e8e1db;background:#faf7f4;padding:15px 18px;margin-bottom:20px">
    <div style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:9.5px;letter-spacing:.14em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#a9a1a1;margin-bottom:7px">3 firm analyte limits across 2 subcategories so far</div>
    <p style="font-size:13px;line-height:1.65;color:#a9a1a1;margin:0">Every row below is locked and has its own page. An analyte renders a firm limit only once it clears all five shipping gates; a row with no firm cell yet is in force as a page but shows its per-analyte state as in development. The standard widens as the evidence base and governance review clear more cells.</p>
