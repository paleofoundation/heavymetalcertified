<section id="method" style="margin-bottom:52px"><h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">How every number on this page is set</h2>
<p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#5a4141;margin:0 0 20px;max-width:84ch;padding:13px 16px;background:#fdf4f3;border-left:3px solid #9f2727"><span style="display:block;font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:10px;letter-spacing:.1em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#9f2727;margin-bottom:5px">What this standard covers</span>Covers teethers and teething toys a child mouths. General children’s toys without a mouthing-specific role are certified under Category 21 (Children’s Toys).</p>
<p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">Teething toys/teethers is a contact product, not a food. Its heavy-metal limits are element migration limits from the material a child mouths (EU <a href="https://heavymetalindex.com/regulations/eu-2009-48-toy-safety-migration-limits" style="color:#1c43ad">Toy Safety Directive 2009/48/EC</a> Category III scraped-off; <a href="https://heavymetalindex.com/regulations/astm-f963-soluble-migration" style="color:#1c43ad">ASTM F963</a>; the EN 71-3 test standard) &mdash; not dietary maximum levels. The standard for each analyte is the strictest limit set by any credible government regulator for this product in its own basis: a lookup of sovereign law, with the measured quantity stated on every row. Where no instrument directly governs the exact product form &mdash; a pacifier is excluded from the Toy Safety Directive, a disposable diaper from the REACH textile restriction &mdash; the limit is read across from the nearest applicable sovereign limit and labelled as a read-across, never asserted as direct governance. Migration limits (what leaves the article into saliva or food) and total-content limits (how much metal the article contains) are different quantities measured by different methods; they are published as separate ceilings and never combined into one number. A certified product meets every applicable limit at once, each on a like-for-like basis.</p>
<p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">Inorganic arsenic and methylmercury are not published for this category: they are ingestion-and-bioaccumulation speciation forms with no migration counterpart in a contact article, and the total-element ceilings (total arsenic, total mercury) bound them. Where a cell is marked not material, no regulator sets a limit and no exposure pathway exists; the determination is recorded, not left blank.</p></section>

<p style="font-size:11.5px;line-height:1.55;color:#a9a1a1;max-width:84ch;margin:0 0 14px">Limits in mg/kg, in the basis named on each row. &ldquo;ND&rdquo; means not detectable at the regulation&rsquo;s stated detection limit &mdash; the strictest possible ceiling. Antimony is regulated in the toy-migration instruments and screened, but is not one of the ten HMTc certification analytes.</p>
