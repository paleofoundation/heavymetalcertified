Aluminium in honey is low natural/environmental background. The best modern reference-method source (naccari2025, ICP-MS, n=38) measures honey Al at 4.5-6.0 mg/kg, consistent with the wider literature (typical 0.1-5 mg/kg; country maxima ~29). The pooled p97 of ~152 mg/kg is a method-inflation artifact, not ubiquitous mineral content: the pool is dominated by an ICP-OES source (tomczyk2020) and reconstruction tails, and honey is not among EFSA's high-Al foods. No credible regulator sets an aluminium ML for honey; dietary Al is TWI-managed. The pool requires reference-method restriction (logged as an outstanding item); the not-material determination rests on the ~5 mg/kg reference value, not the inflated 152.
