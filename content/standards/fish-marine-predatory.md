## Complete analyte panel

Eight total-element results are required: Pb, Cd, total arsenic, total mercury, Ni, Sn, Al, and total Cr. Inorganic arsenic and Cr(VI) are additional required species decisions; methylmercury is conditional. Species never replace the parent total result.

<section id="controls">

## Mandatory controls and species decisions

A nonnumeric cell does not remove the analyte from testing. It records the governed control used when no defensible sovereign numeric maximum exists for the exact cell.

| Analyte | Variant | Requirement and rationale | Reference method or basis | Bounded by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Methylmercury (MeHg) | — | Methylmercury is a component of total mercury (MeHg ≤ tHg). No government sets a methylmercury limit for this product below the binding total-mercury ceiling (1000 µg/kg), so methylmercury is governed by and bounded by the total-mercury ceiling; no separate methylmercury ceiling is published. | Use the category laboratory method on the stated native basis. | tHg |
| Tin (Sn) | — | Tin is a can-migration analyte only: it enters food by dissolution from tinplate can walls, and fresh, frozen, or otherwise non-canned predatory fish (whole, fillet, loin, or portion) has no tin source. No government sets a tin ML for non-canned fish anywhere in the register; every seafood tin limit is scoped to canned/tinplate product and is excluded by the compiler's canned-gate for this non-canned subcategory. Canned predatory-fish products are certified under the canned-seafood subcategory, where the governing tin ceiling is the canned-food ML (Codex CXS 193 / GSO / India canned foods 250000 ug/kg; Chile canned fish and shellfish 250000 ug/kg). For non-canned product, tin is controlled by screen, not limited: any result above the reporting limit is investigated as evidence of unintended metal contact rather than an inherent property of the fish. | Total Sn by ICP-MS or ICP-OES (e.g. AOAC 985.16 / EN 15764); screen threshold for non-canned product set at the method LOQ, with any quantifiable tin triggering a packaging/contact-material investigation. | — |
| Hexavalent chromium (Cr-VI) | — | Total chromium is measured on every lot. Any total-Cr result at or above 100 ug/kg triggers Cr(VI) speciation, and any quantifiable Cr(VI) fails certification. | Total Cr by ICP-MS; Cr(VI) by EPA 3060A alkaline extraction with IC-ICP-MS (EPA 6800 / EN 15192 or equivalent). | Cr |

<section id="method">

## How these cells are set

The initial HMTc value is the strictest applicable maximum level set by a credible sovereign government in the product's native basis. If no government regulates the exact cell, the method uses a disclosed read-across from the nearest applicable sovereign value or an explicit nonnumeric control with its laboratory method. Public occurrence data describe markets and research gaps; they do not select the initial certification value. The 8:1 powder-to-liquid conversion is confined to infant formula.

</section>
