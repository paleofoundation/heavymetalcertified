<section style="margin-bottom:40px"><h1 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:38px;line-height:1.1;color:#2b1f1f;margin:0 0 12px">Standards completeness audit</h1><p style="font-size:16px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0;max-width:84ch">A standard is complete only when its Master Limit Table publishes a numeric limit for Pb, Cd, total As, inorganic As, total Hg, Ni, Sn, Al, total Cr, and Cr(VI), in the product row’s native basis. Methylmercury may be an additional category requirement, but it does not replace total mercury. Regulatory silence, monitoring language, reflex testing, and “not material” do not satisfy a missing limit.</p></section>
<section id="method-audit" style="margin-bottom:52px"><h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">Method audit</h2><p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">All 26 standards in this preview declare a government-limit-default method; 10 of those also mention selective occurrence pooling. The locked HMTc standards math, however, sets clean-subcategory limits at pooled P97 and dirty-subcategory limits at pooled P45, then caps the result at the lowest applicable loaded regulatory ceiling in the row’s native basis. The public method text and the current generators therefore require governance reconciliation before the figures can be represented as one system-wide method.</p><p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">The public method pages also describe the dirty-subcategory P45 outcome as roughly 40% and cite a 42% validation result, while the governing target is approximately 30% joint row pass. That is a published numeric conflict: the target, validation population, and calculation need to be reconciled before either percentage is presented as the governing figure.</p><p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0;max-width:84ch">This matters most in unregulated cells: the program’s own structure page says occurrence math applies where government supplies no number, while 13 current table cells use “not material” instead. Those cells are flagged as unresolved, not exempted.</p></section>
