<section id="method" style="margin-bottom:52px"><h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">How every number on this page is set</h2><p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">The initial standard for each analyte is the strictest applicable maximum level set by a credible sovereign government &mdash; among them the European Union, Codex Alimentarius member governments, the US FDA, Australia/New Zealand, the Gulf states (GSO), and national authorities that regulate the product &mdash; expressed in Rice — bulk grain (white, brown, wild)&rsquo;s own as-sold basis. The 8:1 powder-to-liquid conversion is confined to infant formula. A certified product must also comply with every law in its markets of sale.</p><p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">Where no government regulates the exact product-analyte cell, a firm starting value requires disclosed read-across from the nearest applicable sovereign value or an n/m determination with its control method. Heavy Metal Index occurrence evidence does not select the initial value. After publication, the <a href="/program/ratchet" style="color:#1c43ad;text-decoration:none">ALARA Standards Ratchet</a> may lower it from the sovereign anchor when eligible HMTc certified-lot results meet the published two-cycle trigger.</p></section>

Tier 2 (tAs, Cr-VI, Ni, Sn, Al) carry a 150% transitional allowance under the program&rsquo;s Status A&ndash;E framework. Inorganic arsenic (iAs) is the arsenic species that binds on rice: rice hyperaccumulates arsenic, and every government that regulates it does so by product form (milled 150, parboiled/husked/rice-flour 250, rice products 300, rice drinks 30). Non-rice cereals do not hyperaccumulate arsenic, so iAs is not-material there and total arsenic (tAs) carries the government cereal ceiling instead. Mercury is limited as total mercury (tHg); methylmercury (MeHg), which no government regulates in grain, is bounded by the total-mercury ceiling. Hexavalent chromium (Cr-VI) is controlled by reflex speciation on total chromium, not an invented number. Tin (Sn) is material only for canned product, which routes to the canned-goods category.
