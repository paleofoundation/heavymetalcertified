<section id="method" style="margin-bottom:52px"><h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">How every number on this page is set</h2>
<p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#5a4141;margin:0 0 20px;max-width:84ch;padding:13px 16px;background:#fdf4f3;border-left:3px solid #9f2727"><span style="display:block;font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:10px;letter-spacing:.1em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#9f2727;margin-bottom:5px">What this standard covers</span>Covers the diaper and its structural components: nonwoven topsheet and backsheet, the absorbent core, elastics, adhesives, and closures/fasteners. It does NOT cover baby or diaper wipes, or diaper creams, which are leave-on skin-cleansing and skin-care products certified under Category 2 (Infant and Child Personal Care), rows Baby wipes and Diaper cream.</p>
<p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">Diapers and diaper components is a contact product, not a food. Its heavy-metal limits are extractable-metal limits for the skin-contact textile and elastic components (<a href="https://heavymetalindex.com/regulations/eu-reach-2018-1513-cmr-textiles" style="color:#1c43ad">EU REACH Annex XVII entry 72</a>). Entry 72 excludes disposable textiles, so the limits bind reusable-cloth components directly and are read across as the benchmark for disposable-diaper skin-contact components; the absorbent core has no metal-migration pathway &mdash; not dietary maximum levels. The standard for each analyte is the strictest limit set by any credible government regulator for this product in its own basis: a lookup of sovereign law, with the measured quantity stated on every row. Where no instrument directly governs the exact product form &mdash; a pacifier is excluded from the <a href="https://heavymetalindex.com/regulations/eu-2009-48-toy-safety-migration-limits" style="color:#1c43ad">Toy Safety Directive</a>, a disposable diaper from the REACH textile restriction &mdash; the limit is read across from the nearest applicable sovereign limit and labelled as a read-across, never asserted as direct governance. Migration limits (what leaves the article into saliva or food) and total-content limits (how much metal the article contains) are different quantities measured by different methods; they are published as separate ceilings and never combined into one number. A certified product meets every applicable limit at once, each on a like-for-like basis.</p>
<p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">Inorganic arsenic and methylmercury are not published for this category: they are ingestion-and-bioaccumulation speciation forms with no migration counterpart in a contact article, and the total-element ceilings (total arsenic, total mercury) bound them. Where a cell is marked not material, no regulator sets a limit and no exposure pathway exists; the determination is recorded, not left blank.</p></section>
