<section id="scope">

## Product scope and basis

<p>Diapers and diaper components is evaluated on its extractable metal after extraction (mg/kg homogeneous material) basis.</p>

<p>Covers the diaper and its structural components: nonwoven topsheet and backsheet, the absorbent core, elastics, adhesives, and closures/fasteners. It does NOT cover baby or diaper wipes, or diaper creams, which are leave-on skin-cleansing and skin-care products certified under Category 2 (Infant and Child Personal Care), rows Baby wipes and Diaper cream.</p>
<p>skin-contact extractable metals (<a href="https://heavymetalindex.com/regulations/eu-reach-2018-1513-cmr-textiles" style="color:#1c43ad">EU REACH Annex XVII entry 72</a>). Entry 72 excludes disposable textiles (paragraph 1(b)), so its 1 mg/kg limits bind reusable-cloth diaper components directly and are read across as the benchmark for disposable-diaper skin-contact components; the absorbent core has no metal-migration pathway</p>

</section>

<section id="method">

## How these cells are set

The initial HMTc value is the strictest applicable maximum level set by a credible sovereign government in the product's native basis. If no government regulates the exact cell, the method uses a disclosed read-across from the nearest applicable sovereign value or an explicit nonnumeric control with its laboratory method. Public occurrence data describe markets and research gaps; they do not select the initial certification value. The 8:1 powder-to-liquid conversion is confined to infant formula.
