The register lists every published standard on its own page. This finder is the guided way in: name your product, and read the number it will be held to before you spend anything on testing. It reports what the register holds, it does not decide certification.

You will see whether a standard exists for your product, the product basis it uses, the analytes it evaluates, the published limits, whether the standard is fully in force yet, the tightest limits and where the work usually is, and whether your existing evidence points toward Direct Certification or toward the Readiness Track. No company name is required to look one up, and nothing you select is submitted or stored.
