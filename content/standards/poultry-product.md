<section id="method" class="hmtc-section"><h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px">How limits are set</h2><p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">Category-specific method notes. (1) MUSCLE MEAT vs OFFAL. Every credible instrument writes separate and materially looser maximum levels for edible offal, because lead and cadmium concentrate in liver and kidney.</p><p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">Every row here is scoped to muscle meat and attached edible fat; offal of the same species is a separately-tested sub-lot assessed against that species' offal maximum levels, and no offal value anchors a muscle-meat row. The taxonomy boundary rule places offal inside the animal's own subcategory, which is honoured by sub-lot testing rather than by publishing an offal-derived ceiling.</p><p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">(2) THE CATEGORY 1 BOUNDARY. Products marketed for ages 0-5 route to Category 1 (mixed meals), so the infant and baby-food action levels and maximum levels that would otherwise be the strictest values in the register are excluded by applicability from every row here, and are excluded explicitly in each row's audit_exclude_scope.</p><p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">(3) GAME AND LEAD AMMUNITION. Fragmenting lead projectiles are a documented, mechanical lead pathway in wild-harvested game. That evidence drives mandatory radiographic fragment screening before grinding and priority surveillance on the game row; it does not and cannot move the number, which remains a disclosed read-across from the domestic-mammal muscle-meat maximum level.</p><p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">(4) BASIS TREATMENT ON THE THREE COMPOSITE ROWS, DISCLOSED IN THE DIRECTION IT CUTS. On the processed-meat, canned-meat and analogue rows the component maximum level is applied to the finished product as sold, with no invented conversion factor. This config does NOT claim that treatment is conservative, because it is not: a formulation that is part water, brine, starch or rusk can carry a higher concentration in its meat or protein fraction than the component maximum level itself allows.</p><p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">The gap is closed by a second, independent obligation rather than by a conversion factor. Certification of those three rows requires documented compliance of the raw material or source commodity against the same named component maximum levels, in their own basis, IN ADDITION to finished-product compliance against the published ceiling; neither test substitutes for the other.</p><p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">The eggs row runs the opposite way and is treated accordingly: dried and powdered egg is concentrated relative to whole egg, so applying the whole-egg maximum level to the powder as sold is the STRICTER treatment, and it is applied without being reversed.</p><p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">(5) REGISTER GAPS ARE DISCLOSED, NOT FILLED. The EU 2023/915 and Codex CXS 193 lead rows for meat and offal, the EU and Codex cadmium offal rows, and the FSANZ cadmium meat and offal rows are absent from this register. They are recorded as register gaps for ingestion through the verified regulatory-fetch pathway and are NOT written into this config from memory. No absent row is assigned a remembered value anywhere in this config.</p><p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">Because the method selects the strictest applicable value, ingesting a missing row can only lower a published cell or leave it unchanged, never raise it; every affected cell must be re-derived when the missing rows land.</p></section>

<section id="occurrence-derivation" class="hmtc-section"><h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px">Occurrence and derivation rationale</h2><p style="font-size:13.5px;line-height:1.7;color:#736b6b;max-width:84ch;margin:0 0 22px">For each analyte on the panel: what the peer-reviewed and survey literature measured in this category, cited one way to the Heavy Metal Index, and the Method v2.0 reasoning behind the certification limit — including, where no government sets a number, why the program sets one anyway. Occurrence figures are literature findings; the derivation is the certifier's.</p><div style="margin:0 0 26px;padding:2px 0 2px 16px;border-left:2px solid #e4dcd5"><div style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:11px;letter-spacing:.07em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#9a8f8f;margin:0 0 8px">Lead</div><p style="font-size:14.5px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;max-width:84ch;margin:0 0 10px">1 study in the certified-market pool measured lead in Poultry across 166 certified-market samples. No credible sovereign government sets an inorganic-arsenic maximum level for poultry muscle meat, skin or edible fat. Every credible inorganic-arsenic instrument on the register is scoped elsewhere: EU 2023/915 and EU 2025/1891 to rice, rice products, fruit juices, infant food and seafood; UK, Health Canada and US FDA to rice-based and juice matrices; Hong Kong Cap. 132V to rice and aquatic animals. A NOT-MATERIAL determination is deliberately REJECTED for this cell rather than adopted for convenience: organoarsenical feed additives (roxarsone, nitarsone, arsanilic acid, carbarsone) were lawfully administered to poultry and swine in major producing markets within living memory and are documented to leave arsenic residues in edible tissue, so the programme will not make the affirmative claim that no inorganic-arsenic pathway exists in terrestrial animal food. For the ruminant rows, where no equivalent additive history applies, the absence of that pathway does not by itself substantiate a not-material determination either, because neither a sovereign number nor a supportable near-neighbour analogue exists for the cell in any case. Because no sovereign number governs the cell and no near-neighbour cell is a supportable analogue (a rice or seafood inorganic-arsenic maximum level describes a different contamination mechanism entirely), the cell is a control: testing is mandatory and no ceiling is invented. The analyte is controlled by Inorganic arsenic by anion-exchange HPLC-ICP-MS speciation. Speciation is reflex-triggered on every lot whose total-arsenic screen is quantifiable above the method limit of quantitation, and unconditionally on the first lot from any new supplier, slaughter establishment, or country of origin. Any quantifiable inorganic arsenic is escalated to the certification committee together with the feed-additive and origin declaration; the lot cannot be released on an inorganic-arsenic result while the escalation is open, and a total-arsenic result at or above the binding total-arsenic maximum level is a hard stop irrespective of speciation, not a standalone number. <a href="/analytes/arsenic" First, the reference-method evidence behind the reduction argument does not reach this matrix. That argument (Cr(VI) is reduced to Cr(III) by tissue reductants and does not persist, so a reported Cr(VI) figure is an artifact of the extraction chemistry) rests on saraiva2021, species-specific isotope-dilution HPLC-ICP-MS, in which Cr(VI) was unquantified in every sample and spiked Cr(VI) was reduced essentially completely during workup. A not-material claim is an affirmative claim and may not be extended by analogy to a matrix the underlying method study never examined; it continues to support the beef, pork and lamb rows, where the matrix was directly measured. The paper and attributes them to a documented mechanical supply-chain pathway: chrome-tanned leather offcuts fed to poultry as cheap protein, carrying residual hexavalent chromium from K2CrO4 and K2Cr2O7 tanning liquors. The routed occurrence pool for poultry-product|Cr-VI is not quarantined and carries a stable sensitivity band. The programme records openly that mahmud2011 is weak evidence of SPECIATION: muffle-furnace ashing and nitric-acid digestion followed by atomic absorption calibrated against potassium dichromate, with no chromatographic separation and no speciation-preservation validation, measures total chromium irrespective of oxidation state, and the reported values sit in the same range as the total-chromium screen, which is what a total-chromium measurement mislabelled as Cr(VI) would look like. That caveat is why this cell does not publish a number derived from those values, and it could not in any case: occurrence never selects an HMTc limit. But the FEED-INPUT PATHWAY the paper documents is not an analytical artifact, and the correct response to a real pathway measured by an inadequate method is to require an adequate method, not to declare the analyte not material. Under the programme's fail-safe rule a cell in this position is a control. The analyte is controlled by Hexavalent chromium determined on every certified lot by alkaline-extraction ion chromatography with isotope-dilution ICP-MS (EN 15192, or EPA 3060A extraction with EPA 6800 speciated isotope-dilution mass spectrometry).
