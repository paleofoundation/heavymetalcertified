<section id="method" style="margin-bottom:52px"><h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">How limits are set</h2><p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">Two boundary decisions in this category are disclosed rather than assumed. FIRST, breath fresheners split by presentation: the row certifies cosmetic-presentation forms (sprays, strips, films, non-food oral tablets) on the cosmetic instrument family, and food-presented mints, gums and confectionery lozenges are routed out to Category 33 rather than certified against a cosmetic ceiling: the relevant food instrument (the FDA lead action level for candy likely to be frequently consumed by small children, 100 ug/kg) is five times stricter than this category's lead value, so the routing is in the protective direction.</p><p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">SECOND, articles are not stretched onto cosmetic limits: a toothbrush, a floss, an irrigator tip and a tongue scraper each take a real article-scoped sovereign lead ceiling and a full-panel component-level control, and never a toothpaste value.</p><p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">Total chromium is a control rather than a published screen value in every row because no sovereign publishes a chromium content limit for any product form in this category; hexavalent chromium is deliberately NOT registered as a method artifact here, because oral-care matrices routinely contain peroxide and persulfate oxidising systems in which the reduction of Cr(VI) to Cr(III) cannot be assumed.</p><p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">Tin and aluminium are controls with declaration reconciliation rather than impurity ceilings, because stannous fluoride is an authorised active and hydrated alumina is a conventional abrasive, so both elements are present by design in compliant products.</p></section>
