<section id="method" class="hmtc-section"><h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px">How limits are set</h2><p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">The standard for each analyte is the strictest maximum level set by any credible government regulator &mdash; the European Union, the United Kingdom, Codex Alimentarius, Health Canada, China, India, Brazil, the Gulf states (GSO), Australia/New Zealand, and the US FDA &mdash; converted into Infant formula (powder)&rsquo;s own as-sold basis. It is a lookup of sovereign law, carrying at most one disclosed basis conversion where the law is written in another basis, and an explicit read-across where no government regulates the exact product. A certified product sits at or below every applicable sovereign limit at once.</p><p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">Where <em>no</em> government regulates the exact product for an analyte, the standard does not go blank or invent a number: the limit is read across from the nearest government value for an analogous product, so a certified product still sits at or below a real sovereign limit. The ratchet tightens these after publication as surveillance warrants.</p><div style="margin:4px 0 0"><a href="/static/standards/infant-formula-powder.pdf" style="display:inline-block;background:#9f2727;color:#fff;font-family:&#39;IBM Plex Mono&#39;,monospace;font-size:12px;letter-spacing:.04em;text-decoration:none;padding:11px 18px;border-radius:4px">&#8595; Download this standard (PDF, v3.1)</a> <span style="font-size:11.5px;color:#a9a1a1">Versioned, citable, self-contained.</span></div></section>
