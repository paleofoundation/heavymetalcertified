<section id="scope">

## Product scope and basis

<p>Pacifiers and sucking/teething aids is evaluated on its element migration from soother material (EN 1400 / EN 71-3 Category III, mg/kg) basis.</p>

<p>Covers soothers/pacifiers and sucking/teething aids (the nipple, shield, ring, and any clip in the mouthing zone). Teething toys with play value are certified under Teething toys/teethers; pacifier metal safety is governed via the EN 1400 soother standard.</p>
<p>EN 1400 soother standard, which requires the EN 71-3 / <a href="https://heavymetalindex.com/regulations/eu-2009-48-toy-safety-migration-limits" style="color:#1c43ad">Directive 2009/48/EC</a> Annex II Part III Category III element-migration limits. Pacifiers are excluded from the Toy Safety Directive itself (Annex I item 16), so the migration limits are read across from the toy-migration regime via the EN 1400 route; the limit values are identical</p>

</section>

## Mandatory controls and species decisions

A nonnumeric cell does not remove the analyte from testing. It records the governed control used when no defensible sovereign numeric maximum exists for the exact cell.

| Analyte | Variant | Requirement and rationale | Reference method or basis | Bounded by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Methylmercury (MeHg) | — | Methylmercury is a marine-bioaccumulation speciation with no formation or migration pathway in a pacifier or teether. <section id="method">

## How these cells are set

The initial HMTc value is the strictest applicable maximum level set by a credible sovereign government in the product's native basis. If no government regulates the exact cell, the method uses a disclosed read-across from the nearest applicable sovereign value or an explicit nonnumeric control with its laboratory method. Public occurrence data describe markets and research gaps; they do not select the initial certification value. The 8:1 powder-to-liquid conversion is confined to infant formula.
