<section id="method" style="margin-bottom:52px"><h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">How limits are set</h2><p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">No government sets a heavy-metal content limit for this class. Peptides, SARMs, and research chemicals are sold outside any drug-approval or dietary-supplement framework, so the sovereign maximum-level lookup that anchors every other HMTc standard returns nothing here. HMTc does not answer that gap by inventing a number. Every analyte on the panel is placed under mandatory control, measured on every lot by validated ICP-MS with reflex inorganic-arsenic speciation, and escalated against route-appropriate toxicological anchors (ICH Q3D / USP &lt;232&gt; permitted daily exposures), which flag a lot for investigation and are never used as a product-content ceiling.</p><p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">Certification here is heavy-metal safety only. HMTc does not endorse, approve, or vouch for the efficacy or the legality of these products; the mark speaks solely to the absence of a detectable heavy-metal burden on the tested lot. Where the chemistry rules an analyte out of the matrix entirely, it is recorded as a substantiated not-material determination rather than a control.</p></section>
