## Complete analyte panel

<section id="controls">

## Mandatory controls and species decisions

A nonnumeric cell does not remove the analyte from testing. It records the governed control used when no defensible sovereign numeric maximum exists for the exact cell.

| Analyte | Variant | Requirement and rationale | Reference method or basis | Bounded by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Inorganic arsenic (iAs) | — | Cannabis regulations set arsenic limits without speciation; these are recorded as total arsenic. Total As is measured on every lot and bounds the inorganic fraction; any elevated result triggers iAs speciation. | Total As by ICP-MS; inorganic arsenic by HPLC-ICP-MS on an elevated total-As result. | tAs |
| Methylmercury (MeHg) | — | Cannabis has no methylmercury bioaccumulation pathway. Total Hg is measured on every lot and bounds the methylmercury fraction. | Total Hg by EPA 7473 or ICP-MS; reflex MeHg speciation only on an anomalous total-Hg result. | tHg |
| Nickel (Ni) | — | Nickel is a mandatory total-panel result because cannabis can accumulate Ni and inhalation hardware can release it, but no loaded sovereign cannabis rule sets a finished-product total-Ni maximum. Report every result, investigate source and route, and do not represent this cell as a numeric government limit. | Total Ni by ICP-MS; for inhaled products, aerosol-emissions testing separates plant/extract contamination from hardware release. | — |
| Tin (Sn) | — | No loaded sovereign cannabis rule sets a total-tin maximum. Total Sn remains mandatory surveillance, with any quantifiable result investigated for packaging, solder, catalyst, or device contact. | Total Sn by ICP-MS; organotin speciation when the formulation, elastomer, catalyst, or packaging pathway makes organotin plausible. | — |
| Aluminium (Al) | — | No loaded sovereign cannabis rule sets a total-aluminium maximum. Total Al remains mandatory surveillance and elevated results trigger soil, processing-aid, excipient, or device-source investigation. | Total Al by ICP-MS with route-appropriate blank and device controls. | — |
| Hexavalent chromium (Cr-VI) | — | Total chromium is mandatory. Inhaled products and any anomalous total-Cr result require Cr(VI) speciation; any quantifiable Cr(VI) fails certification. | Total Cr by ICP-MS; Cr(VI) by validated species-specific IC-ICP-MS with matrix-specific recovery controls. | Cr |

</section>

<section id="method">

## How these cells are set

The initial HMTc value is the strictest applicable maximum level set by a credible sovereign government in the product's native basis. If no government regulates the exact cell, the method uses a disclosed read-across from the nearest applicable sovereign value or an explicit nonnumeric control with its laboratory method. Public occurrence data describe markets and research gaps; they do not select the initial certification value. The 8:1 powder-to-liquid conversion is confined to infant formula.
