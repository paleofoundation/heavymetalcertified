<section id="method" style="margin-bottom:52px"><h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">How limits are set</h2><p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">The standard for each analyte is the strictest maximum level set by any credible government regulator, among them the European Union, Codex Alimentarius, the US FDA, Australia/New Zealand, the Gulf states (GSO), and the national authorities of every jurisdiction that regulates the product, converted into Vinegar (distilled, wine, balsamic, cider, rice)&rsquo;s own as-consumed basis. It is a lookup of sovereign law, carrying at most one disclosed basis conversion where the law is written in another basis, and an explicit read-across where no government regulates the exact product. A certified product sits at or below every applicable sovereign limit at once.</p><p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:84ch">Where <em>no</em> government regulates the exact product for an analyte, the standard does not go blank or invent a number: the limit is read across from the nearest government value for an analogous product, so a certified product still sits at or below a real sovereign limit. The ratchet tightens these after publication as surveillance warrants.</p></section>

<section style="margin-bottom:52px"><h2 id="risk-flag" style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">Risk Flag</h2><p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;max-width:74ch;margin:0 0 22px">Where certification is demanding for this subcategory, and why. These carry a natural or process background that runs above the limit: the limit being the strictest government maximum for the product, never a percentile of market data (Method v2.0). A product is not disqualified by this: only its own tested result decides certification, and a well-sourced lot can sit well below the market.</p><div class="hmtc-mhard-list"><details class="hmtc-mhard" open>
<summary class="hmtc-mhard-sum"><span class="hmtc-mhard-sign" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="hmtc-mhard-name">Aluminium</span></summary>
<div class="hmtc-mhard-panel">
<p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.76;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:78ch">Grains and cereals, aluminium-based leavening agents (baking powder made with sodium aluminium sulfate or phosphate), and some tablet and capsule excipients carry high aluminium.
