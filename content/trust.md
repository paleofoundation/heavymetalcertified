Trust in a certification program does not come from saying that the system is independent. It comes from showing which institution performs which function, which information can cross each boundary, which rules are published before a result, and which claims the program refuses to make.

## Institutional roles

| Institution or actor | Function | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Heavy Metal Index | Public evidence record for regulations, occurrence evidence, methods, analytical fitness, uncertainty, version history, and corrections | Does not certify products, endorse applicants, or receive private certification files. |
| Institute of Contaminant Standards | Publishes and administers the program architecture, standards governance, manuals, and institutional controls | Must keep evidence governance and commercial certification decisions distinguishable and auditable. |
| Heavy Metal Tested & Certified | Applies the governing standard and program rules to a specific product, issues certification status, licenses the mark, and conducts surveillance | Does not turn a product-level result into a blanket brand, safety, or zero-metals claim. |
| Accredited laboratory | Produces analytical results using methods and scope appropriate to the analyte and matrix | Does not set the certification standard or decide the commercial meaning of the mark. |
| Applicant or certificate holder | Supplies accurate product, formula, lot, supplier, sample, and change information and complies with legal and program duties | Payment and participation do not create a right to a passing decision. |

The evidence citation runs one way: HMTc may cite the public Heavy Metal Index. HMI does not endorse HMTc, an applicant, or a certified product.

## The certification and HMI firewall

| Information | Permitted destination | Prohibited use |
|---|---|---|
| Public regulations, studies, surveys, and analytical sources | HMI and, by citation, HMTc | Reframing a certification client's private data as public evidence. |
| Private application, formulation, supplier, and readiness records | Controlled certification file | Entry into HMI or publication as category evidence without a separately governed lawful basis. |
| Private laboratory results and CAPA records | Controlled certification and surveillance file | Disclosure as another brand's benchmark or supplier intelligence. |
| Public certificate status and scope | Verification surface | Implying that nonlisted products, prior lots, other forms, or the entire brand are certified. |
| Aggregated program information | Only under a separately defined aggregation, privacy, and disclosure rule | Publishing a group so small or detailed that a participant can be identified. |

A certification client must not be able to suppress, delay, rewrite, or veto a public HMI conclusion merely because the conclusion is commercially inconvenient. Conversely, an HMI editor should not have access to a private certification file merely because the same parent institution operates both systems.

The complete operational firewall still requires a ratified data-governance policy covering role-based access, retention, deletion, legal holds, compulsory process, regulator requests, aggregation, and audit logging. The website should not imply that architecture exists beyond what the contracts and systems actually enforce.

## Fees do not buy a passing decision

The standard is published before a brand submits a result. The applicable product form, analytes, basis, decision rules, status rules, and mark scope are not negotiated after the number is known.

A program fee pays for evaluation and program services. It does not purchase a certification outcome. Formal separation of sales, technical review, certification decision, conflicts, compensation, recusal, and appeals should be documented and auditable. Until those controls are ratified, the site should not make a stronger organizational-independence claim than the operating record supports.

## Data confidentiality and legal obligations

Private readiness and certification records can be protected as confidential business information under contract and access controls. That is not the same as attorney-client privilege, work-product protection, or immunity from legal process.

The program does not promise to conceal information from a regulator, delay a recall, override a reporting duty, defeat a subpoena, avoid a legal hold, or relieve a brand of a contractual notice obligation. The brand remains responsible for its own legal advice and compliance.

## What the certification establishes

The mark is a product-specific statement that the named product was evaluated under the named standard, edition, panel, basis, period, and program rules. The certificate and verification record should make that scope checkable.

## What the certification does not establish

| Claim not made | Reason |
|---|---|
| Heavy metal free | Analytical testing cannot support absolute absence. |
| No toxic metals | The panel, methods, and quantitation limits have defined scope. |
| Safe under every use condition | Certification is not a complete individual or population risk assessment. |
| Every product from the brand is certified | Certification is product and form specific. |
| Every past or future lot conforms | Certification and surveillance are time- and evidence-bounded. |
| Private records are legally privileged | Contractual confidentiality and legal privilege are different doctrines. |
| HMI is equivalent to the Cochrane Library | HMI may be built around systematic-review principles, but equivalence requires mature methods, governance, audit, and external scrutiny. |
| The certification body holds an accreditation not actually awarded | Laboratory accreditation and certification-scheme accreditation are separate claims. |
| Certification removes regulatory duties | A private mark cannot displace law, regulator authority, or contractual obligations. |

## Heavy Metal Index as the evidence engine

The defensible aspiration is not merely to collect more citations. It is to create a living evidence system in which an important number can be traced backward through a reproducible search, source record, inclusion decision, analytical-fitness assessment, extraction, synthesis, uncertainty statement, reviewer action, version, and correction.

HMI can credibly describe itself as being built around systematic-review principles when those controls are visible. Claims of Cochrane-level equivalence should wait for registered protocols, comprehensive searches, dual or equivalent independent review, conflict controls, error-rate reporting, correction history, methodological audit, and external scrutiny.

## Corrections, appeals, and versioning

The program should preserve a record of what standard and edition governed each decision. Corrections should be public when they alter public content. Appeals should be reviewed under published rules and should not be decided solely by the people responsible for the appealed determination.

The current stability rule binds each decision to its named edition, prohibits silent retroactivity, and carries existing certifications through a published transition when limits tighten. Each substantive change record still must state its redline, reason, notice, effective date, transition, existing-certificate treatment, retesting, cost treatment, and correction or appeal route.
