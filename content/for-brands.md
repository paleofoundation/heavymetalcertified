## Testing should create a plan, not a trap

Heavy Metal Tested &amp; Certified gives a brand a controlled path from uncertainty to verified conformance. You can read the limit your product will be held to before you apply. You can begin through a nonpublic readiness process before your product is ready to carry the mark. You receive exact quantitative results, not a vague pass-or-fail message. When a correctable result does not meet the HMTc standard, the next step is a defined investigation, a corrective-action plan, and a retest.

Certification begins only when the finished product is ready for independent evaluation.

| Before you begin | HMTc commitment |
|---|---|
| Published target | The applicable limits are available before you test |
| Applicant privacy | HMTc does not publish a list of applicants or unsuccessful applicants |
| Quantitative results | Your authorized team receives the actual measured values |
| Custody of your data | Your raw exploratory results stay with you or your laboratory; HMTc retains the conformity evidence behind each certification decision and does not aggregate raw exploratory test data into a central repository, subject to applicable law and lawful process |
| Independent decision | Remediation assistance does not decide certification |
| Mark integrity | The mark is not awarded until the product independently conforms, and is withdrawn from a product confirmed above its published limit under the published status rules |
| Honest boundaries | Confidentiality does not override a legal, regulatory, recall, or safety obligation |

## You do not need to know that you will pass before you enter

A conventional certification application begins with a high-stakes question: pass or fail. HMTc begins with a more useful one.

> What would this product need in order to become certifiable, and to remain certifiable?

For some products the answer is straightforward. Existing reports may already cover part of the panel, and the product may sit far enough below the applicable limits to proceed directly to certification. For others, the result may point to a supplier, an ingredient, an origin, a process, a package, or a source of lot-to-lot variation that needs to change.

A product that is not certification-ready is not a rejected brand. It is a product that needs a defined reduction pathway before the mark can be awarded.

## Readiness and certification are different activities

### 1. Map the requirement

HMTc identifies the standard that applies to the product in the form in which it is sold. You see the analytes, the limits, the basis, the sampling requirements, the laboratory requirements, and the certification conditions before committing to the official evaluation. Existing laboratory reports are reviewed to reduce duplicated work, and are accepted only where their method, scope, units, laboratory competence, and sample identity are suitable for the decision being made.

### 2. Learn where the product stands

Testing is sequenced according to the product and the question. Where appropriate, readiness work can begin with incoming ingredients, supplier samples, developmental batches, packaging investigations, or held production lots, so that an affected input or lot can be controlled before release. Your authorized team receives the quantitative results and the information needed to interpret them.

### 3. Correct the cause

A result that does not meet an HMTc limit is classified according to its context. A correctable nonconformity can enter a private reduction process, which may include targeted ingredient testing, supplier review, origin comparison, process investigation, formulation assessment, or specification changes, supported by the evidence. The objective is not one favorable retest. It is a product and control system capable of remaining within the limit under ordinary production variation.

### 4. Certify independently

Readiness work does not award the mark. When the product is ready, the official certification evaluation begins under the applicable sampling, chain-of-custody, laboratory, review, and decision rules. The product receives the mark only after the certification evidence demonstrates conformance with every applicable requirement. Certification is followed by surveillance, so the mark continues to represent a current control system rather than a single favorable sample from the past.

## What happens when a result does not meet an HMTc limit

| Situation | HMTc response |
|---|---|
| An incoming ingredient does not conform before use | The ingredient can be rejected, replaced, investigated, or requalified before it enters certified production |
| A developmental or pilot batch does not conform | The formulation or source can be corrected before official certification |
| A finished lot is held and does not conform before release | The lot does not enter the certified stream; the cause is investigated and fresh production is verified |
| A product does not conform during pre-certification readiness | No mark is awarded and no public failed-application entry is created; the product may proceed through remediation and retesting |
| A conforming certified product develops an adverse trend | Preventive corrective action begins before the trend becomes an exceedance |
| A released certified lot is confirmed above its published limit | The affected certificate and mark are handled under the published status rules, and any required legal, regulatory, retailer, withdrawal, or recall action remains fully applicable |
| Evidence may indicate a legal or public-health issue | The result is escalated to your designated quality, food-safety, regulatory, and legal decision-makers; HMTc does not delay or obstruct required action |
| A brand conceals, falsifies, or refuses to address a material finding | The matter becomes an integrity violation and may result in suspension or revocation; where a brand refuses to act on a result HMTc reasonably believes presents a public-health hazard, HMTc may revoke certification and take the further steps available to it |

## What remains nonpublic, and what we hold

HMTc does not publish the identity of a brand merely because it requested a readiness review or began a pre-certification improvement process. Ordinary readiness information, exploratory testing, and corrective-action work remain nonpublic to the extent permitted by the governing agreements and applicable law. HMTc does not maintain a public list of brands that applied but were not yet ready to certify.

A private HMTc nonconformity is not automatically an illegal, dangerous, or recalled product. HMTc limits may be more protective than the legal requirement in a particular market, so a product can miss an HMTc limit while remaining fully compliant with the law.

The custody of exploratory data is deliberate. Your raw readiness results stay with you or your laboratory by default, to the extent permitted by applicable law and the governing agreements, and subject to lawful process. HMTc retains the conformity evidence it needs to make and stand behind a certification decision, and does not aggregate brands' raw exploratory test data into a central repository. Category learning is drawn from de-identified, aggregated data under minimum-cell-size controls, which is a governed method for limiting re-identification risk rather than an absolute promise of anonymity, particularly where a category, origin, or formulation is distinctive.

## What confidentiality does not do

Confidentiality is a commercial and operational control. It is not a promise that relevant evidence can never be discovered, and it is not attorney-client privilege. A brand with a specific legal exposure should structure privileged work through its own counsel.

The Readiness Track does not:

- delay a recall or a withdrawal;
- override a reporting or disclosure duty;
- prevent a brand from meeting a retailer, distributor, insurer, or contractual obligation;
- prevent compliance with an applicable regulatory process;
- permit use of the HMTc mark on a nonconforming product;
- turn a potentially serious result into an ordinary private-standard matter;
- authorize HMTc to make a legal decision for the brand.

The program is designed to find and correct contamination before a product is certified, not to make material facts disappear.

## Why this is more valuable than an isolated laboratory test

| Isolated testing | HMTc |
|---|---|
| Produces a number | Produces a number inside a published decision system |
| May test only the customary four metals | Evaluates the defined HMTc panel for the applicable product |
| May not define what happens next | Establishes the next step before the result is known |
| Often begins with finished product | Can begin upstream during readiness where appropriate |
| May produce no root-cause pathway | Connects the result to a controlled investigation and correction |
| Represents one sample and one day | Continues through certification and surveillance |
| Does not authorize a checkable claim | Can lead to a product-specific, independently verifiable mark |
| Leaves each retailer to interpret the evidence | Produces a controlled certification dossier for due diligence |

## What the mark means

The HMTc mark makes a defined product-level claim. It means that the stated product, in the stated form, was evaluated against the applicable HMTc standard and met the published limits and the other certification requirements in force for that certificate.

It does not mean that the product contains no detectable metals, that it was evaluated for every possible contaminant, that every product made by the same brand is certified, that the product is guaranteed safe for every person or every pattern of consumption, or that the mark replaces regulatory compliance, quality control, or a food-safety program.

The scope is intentionally narrow enough to be checked.

## Before you spend on testing

Begin by finding the standard that applies to your product. You will be able to see whether a standard currently exists, the product basis it uses, the analytes it evaluates, the published limits, the known ingredient or matrix challenges, whether your existing evidence may be relevant, and whether direct certification or the Readiness Track is the more appropriate route. No company name is required to look up a standard.

## Questions brands ask before entering

### Will HMTc publish that we applied?

No public applicant or failed-applicant directory is created. A product becomes public when it is certified, or when an existing public certificate must change status under the published rules.

### What happens if our first result does not conform?

You receive the measured result and the applicable interpretation. If the finding is suitable for ordinary remediation, the product can proceed through investigation, correction, and retesting. The mark is not awarded until the product conforms.

### Will HMTc hide a dangerous result?

No. The program distinguishes an HMTc-standard nonconformity from a result that may require legal, regulatory, contractual, withdrawal, recall, or other safety action. The latter is escalated to your decision-makers rather than hidden.

### Can we enter with laboratory data we already have?

Potentially. Existing reports are reviewed for analytical method, laboratory competence, sample identity, units, limits of quantification, analyte coverage, and suitability for the intended decision. Existing data may reduce duplication, but it does not automatically replace official certification evidence.

### Does applying create a certification claim?

No. Readiness participation is not certification and may not be represented to consumers as certification.

### Does certification guarantee that our product is safe?

No certification can make that unlimited claim. HMTc certifies conformance to a defined product standard and analyte panel. It does not certify every aspect of product safety, nutrition, efficacy, or suitability.

## Begin before the market makes the decision for you

You do not need a perfect product to begin. You need a published target, accurate evidence, a controlled response, and the willingness to correct what the evidence identifies. HMTc provides the route from there to independently verified certification.
