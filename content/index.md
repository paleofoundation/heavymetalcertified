      <h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:40px;line-height:1.15;letter-spacing:-.015em;color:#2b1f1f;margin:0">What this<br />certification is</h2>
      <div style="width:80px;height:1px;background:#9f2727;margin-top:22px"></div>
    </div>
    <div style="min-width:0">
      <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3f;margin:0;max-width:72ch">Heavy Metal Tested &amp; Certified evaluates finished goods against published heavy-metal limits. The <a href="https://contaminantstandards.com" style="color:#1c43ad">Institute of Contaminant Standards (ICS)</a> operates the program. The <a href="https://heavymetalindex.com" style="color:#1c43ad">Heavy Metal Index</a> makes the underlying evidence available to inspect, so a buyer can follow the proof from source to result to product decision.</p>
      <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3f;margin:22px 0 0;max-width:72ch">Certification is specific and evidence-based. To earn the mark, a product is independently tested by a laboratory accredited to ISO/IEC 17025 and shown to sit at or below every published value for its subcategory, on the ten-analyte panel and in its native basis, under ongoing lot surveillance. The mark is granted per product, not brand-wide, so it stands for the exact item that carries it.</p>
      <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3f;margin:22px 0 0;max-width:72ch">The current method starts with the lowest applicable finalized government limit, in the product’s native basis. Where no government limit exists, mandatory read-across carries its source and rationale. The published standard identifies the applicable numbers and their provenance.</p>
