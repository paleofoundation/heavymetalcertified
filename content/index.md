<section id="what" style="max-width:1760px;margin:0 auto;padding:72px 48px 72px" data-no-cite>

    <div>
      <h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:40px;line-height:1.15;letter-spacing:-.015em;color:#2b1f1f;margin:0">What this<br />certification is</h2>
      <div style="width:80px;height:1px;background:#9f2727;margin-top:22px"></div>
    </div>
    <div style="min-width:0">
      <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3f;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">Heavy Metal Tested &amp; Certified publishes numeric heavy-metal limits and certifies products against them. Limits are set per product subcategory, stated in each product&rsquo;s native basis, and derived from a pooled distribution of peer-reviewed and government occurrence data. The program is operated by Paleo Certified, Inc. and governed by the Institute of Contaminant Standards.</p>
      <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3f;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">Most food certifications ask whether a producer followed a process. HMTc asks a narrower question with a checkable answer: was this product tested, at an accredited laboratory, at or below a published number, on a stated analyte panel, in the form it is actually sold in. Every term in that sentence is a variable a reader can look up, and the limits are published before anything is certified against them &mdash; so a brand can read the threshold it will be held to before it applies or spends anything on testing.</p>
      <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3f;margin:0;max-width:72ch">It certifies; it does not reassure. The mark does not assert that a product is safe, that analytes outside the panel were controlled, or that a different form from the same brand was tested. Where the pooled literature does not support a limit as tightly as the program sets it, the <a href="/standards" style="color:#1c43ad">standard names that gap</a> rather than smoothing it.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

A program implying otherwise would be making a claim its evidence cannot support.</p></div></div>

A published value is the lower of that pooled figure and the lowest applicable regulatory ceiling &mdash; sometimes stricter than a government limit, never looser.</p><a href="/program/structure" style="display:inline-block;font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:10px;letter-spacing:.08em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#9f2727;margin-top:13px">How limits are derived &rarr;</a></div></div>
