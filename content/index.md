      <p style="font-size:17px;line-height:1.75;color:#2b1f1f;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">Initial HMTc values begin with the strictest applicable maximum set by a credible sovereign government, expressed in the product&rsquo;s native basis.       <p style="font-size:16px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0;max-width:72ch">Certification attaches to a named product and form under a named standard edition. It does not certify an entire brand, claim that a product is heavy-metal free, or replace legal compliance.</p>
