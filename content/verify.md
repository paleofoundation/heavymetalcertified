The certificate register is the public record of every product certified under the Heavy Metal Tested & Certified program. It is open to anyone, including retailers and regulators, and returns one record at a time. There is no bulk export and no way to enumerate the register; a lookup confirms a single certificate you already hold an identifier for.

## What you can look up

- **Lot number** — the code printed on an individual production lot.
- **Certificate number** — the identifier issued to a certified product.
- **Product code** — a brand's own SKU or GTIN for a certified product.

## What a certificate names

A certificate names the standard edition a product was tested against, the analytes measured, and the product's current status. The program's statuses, gates, and remediation pathways are described in the [program structure](/program/structure) and the [remediation pathways](/program/two-pathways).

## Register status

The register opens when the first certificate issues. Until then, no certificates are in force, and this page describes how verification will work rather than serving live lookups.
