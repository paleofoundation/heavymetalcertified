## Abstract

A verification-first review of the Gulf and Asian market-access claims in the 2026 roundup produces an uneven but actionable map. China officially published GB 2762-2025 with a 2 September 2026 effective date, making it a near-term successor rather than a current rule before that date. Korea's MFDS Notice 2025-56 adds a cadmium criterion for cocoa and chocolate. Taiwan has an official amended analytical method, and Hong Kong's expanded Cap. 132V schedule is operating with a grace period for pre-existing foods. The UK-EU SPS project adds a separate alignment pathway relevant to companies using Great Britain as a market or distribution base. By contrast, claimed details for GSO 2783, a specific 2026 Indian amendment, a Vietnam amendment and a 2025 Thailand food-contact change remain partial or unverified. Market readiness should therefore pair confirmed actions with a controlled evidence-gap queue, never convert an inaccessible claim into law, and preserve product, method, species and deadline distinctions.

## China Has a Successor Standard and a Live Pipeline

China's National Health Commission and market regulator officially published GB 2762-2025 and set 2 September 2026 as the date on which it replaces GB 2762-2022 <sup class="cite"><a href="#ref-1">1</a></sup>. Before that date, the successor belongs in readiness planning rather than a current-rule statement. On and after that date, a system still labeling the 2022 text current becomes stale. The HMI record preserves the scheduled supersession and the underlying standard artifact <sup class="cite"><a href="#ref-2">2</a></sup>. Separately, NHC's public consultation register shows an active national food-safety standards pipeline <sup class="cite"><a href="#ref-3">3</a></sup>. The register supports monitoring, but not a claim about a specific draft metal value unless the relevant consultation attachment is identified and verified.

## Korea, Taiwan and Hong Kong Support Immediate Controls

Korea's MFDS official notice identifies a food-code amendment adding a cadmium criterion for cocoa and chocolate <sup class="cite"><a href="#ref-4">4</a></sup>. Taiwan's FDA publishes the amended analytical method used for multiple elements, which can affect the acceptable evidence package even where a product limit is unchanged <sup class="cite"><a href="#ref-5">5</a></sup>. Hong Kong's Centre for Food Safety confirms that the expanded metallic-contamination schedule came into operation on 5 September 2025 and provides a grace period for foods that were already present under the earlier regime <sup class="cite"><a href="#ref-6">6</a></sup>. These events lead to different actions: a cocoa product-row check for Korea, a method and reporting check for Taiwan, and a schedule-plus-transition check for Hong Kong. Combining them into an Asia limit would erase the actual compliance work.

## The Gulf, India, Vietnam and Thailand Require Evidence Triage

The discovery document's GSO 2783 claim could not be reconciled to an accessible official record. HMI already covers the licensed GSO 193:2021 contaminant standard, but that does not prove the identity or legal effect of GSO 2783. India's official 2026 consolidated contaminant compendium is available, while the specific amendment and fish-oil detail described in the roundup were not confirmed. No adequate official instrument was identified for the claimed Vietnam amendment. Thailand has official food-packaging rules, including a plastic-packaging instrument, but the specific 2025 migration change in the roundup was not located <sup class="cite"><a href="#ref-7">7</a></sup>. The correct action is a gap queue naming the missing official record, not a compliance bulletin that converts uncertainty into a requirement.

## UK-EU Alignment Changes the Market-Access Architecture

The UK and EU are negotiating a common sanitary and phytosanitary area built around dynamic alignment. Official UK business guidance says the government is targeting mid-2027 while acknowledging that exact timing and exceptions depend on negotiations <sup class="cite"><a href="#ref-8">8</a></sup>. This is not an adopted list of new contaminant limits. It is an architecture change that may make relevant EU food-safety measures apply in Great Britain and change authorization, border and evidence workflows. A company serving both markets should map which EU contaminant, method, packaging and food-contact instruments fall within the eventual scope, while keeping the future alignment status visible.

## Build a Market File, Not a Regional Spreadsheet

Each target market needs a controlled file with the official instrument, legal status, effective and transition dates, product row, analyte species, method, basis, labeling or disclosure duty and evidence owner. The file should distinguish a verified requirement from a monitor-only signal and an unverified gap. It should link to HMI's neutral instrument record and then to the applicable HMTc standard without copying threshold numbers into multiple local documents. This structure supports action where evidence is strong, preserves readiness where a future date is fixed, and prevents a rumor from becoming an internal specification. It also makes the next update cheap: change the event status once, regenerate the hub and feeds, then trigger governance only if the verified instrument actually changes a binding comparison.

## Frequently asked questions

### When does China GB 2762-2025 take effect?

The official publication sets 2 September 2026 as the effective date. Before that date it is an adopted successor; after that date the status must be updated and the 2022 text treated as superseded.

### Is GSO 2783 verified in this watch?

No. The claimed identity and legal effect were not confirmed from an accessible official instrument. Existing HMI coverage of GSO 193:2021 does not prove the roundup's GSO 2783 claims.

### What changed in Hong Kong?

Hong Kong's expanded Cap. 132V metallic-contamination schedule came into operation on 5 September 2025, with official guidance describing a grace period for pre-existing foods.

### Does a regional roundup prove market access?

No. Market access depends on the official instrument, product scope, analyte species, method, legal status and date in each jurisdiction. Unverified claims stay in a gap queue.
