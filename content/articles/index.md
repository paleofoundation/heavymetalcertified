# Articles

Analysis from the certification program, grounded in the independent evidence base. Every article cites the [Heavy Metal Index](https://heavymetalindex.com) one way: the index reports what the literature says, and these articles apply that evidence to the certification question. The index never cites back, which is what keeps it a disinterested reference.

The full evidence — occurrence distributions, regulatory caps as reported, and the per-metal reviews — lives on the index. These articles link to it rather than re-hosting it.

## Evidence anchors on the index

- **Lead in food** — the occurrence record and the metal review: [heavymetalindex.com/metals/lead](https://heavymetalindex.com/metals/lead)
- **Cadmium** — distribution and exposure: [heavymetalindex.com/metals/cadmium](https://heavymetalindex.com/metals/cadmium)
- **Inorganic arsenic** — speciation and the rice/seaweed signal: [heavymetalindex.com/metals/arsenic](https://heavymetalindex.com/metals/arsenic)
- **Mercury** — total versus methylmercury in seafood: [heavymetalindex.com/metals/mercury](https://heavymetalindex.com/metals/mercury)
