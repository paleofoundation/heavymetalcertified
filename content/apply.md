Certification is open to any product in a subcategory that has a published standard. A brand does not wait for the rest of its category; a product is eligible the moment a standard exists for its subcategory.

## How it works

1. **Read the standard.** Every limit is published before anything is certified against it, so a brand can see the threshold it will be held to — and the analyte panel, and the basis — before it applies or spends anything on testing. Start at the [standards register](/standards).
2. **Test at an accredited laboratory.** Products are quantified by ICP-MS on the ten-analyte panel, in the basis the product is sold in.
3. **Submit results against the published limits.** A product is certified when it is found at or below every value in the standard for its subcategory.

## Fees and terms

Fees are annual and scale with the applicant's gross annual revenue, so a small brand is not priced at an enterprise rate. Multi-year terms are discounted. The full fee schedule and the application form open with the register; until then, direct enquiries through the [Institute of Contaminant Standards](https://contaminantstandards.com).
