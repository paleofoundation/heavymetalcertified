<section id="eligibility" style="margin-bottom:52px">
  <h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">Eligibility</h2>
  <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">A product is eligible when a published subcategory standard exists for its form. Standards publish per subcategory, never as a category-wide release, so one subcategory can be published long before the rest of its category. An application is not charged until the standard for that subcategory publishes.</p>
  <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">A published standard is what a brand needs to begin &mdash; to enter <a href="/category-readiness" style="color:#1c43ad">Private Readiness</a>, size the work, and plan. It is not the same thing as a category being <em style="font-style:normal;color:#2b1f1f">open for certification</em>, which additionally requires the methods, laboratory route, decision rules, agreements, mark controls, surveillance, and verification to be operational for that product form. The <a href="/category-readiness" style="color:#1c43ad">category-readiness map</a> states which categories have reached which state; today every category is at Private Readiness, and none has been declared open for certification.</p>
  <div style="border-left:3px solid #9f2727;background:#fdf4f3;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:20px">
    <p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#5a4141;margin:0">There is no pass-by-declaration route. A brand cannot certify by attesting that it meets the standard; it certifies by testing to it at an accredited laboratory and submitting the results. That is the whole difference between this mark and a self-affixed claim.</p>
  </div>
  <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">HMTc can be held alone or bundled with Certified Paleo, KETO Certified and Grain-Free Certified on a single application. Bundling reduces audit duplication but does not change the analyte panel or the thresholds.</p>
</section>
