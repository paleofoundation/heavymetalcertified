<section class="hmtc-section">
<h2 id="scope" class="hmtc-h2" style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px">Scope and method</h2>
<p class="hmtc-prose" style="font-size:17.5px;line-height:1.72;color:#2b1f1f;margin:0 0 18px">This manual states how Category 21 Children's Toys, Arts, and Crafts is certified for metal safety. It does not certify efficacy, legality, or approval status. Inorganic arsenic is not material on these standards. The cell is not a number. Methylmercury is not material on these standards. The cell is not a number. Hexavalent chromium publishes the number already on the live standard. That number does not replace total chromium. Every figure in the matrix is read from the live standard record. Nothing in this manual invents a ceiling.</p>
<p class="hmtc-prose" style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 4px">Method: <a href="/program/structure" style="color:#1c43ad">government-limit default (Method v2.0)</a>. Why Method v1 was retired is on <a href="/program/method" style="color:#1c43ad">the method page</a>. The twenty-one live standards remain the derivation of each cell. A product is certified only at or below 100% of every published ceiling. The v1 PDF carries the program rules and the standards register, including each house DOI.</p>
</section>
