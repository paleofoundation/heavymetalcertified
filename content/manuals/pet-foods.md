<section class="hmtc-section">
<h2 id="scope" class="hmtc-h2" style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px">Scope and method</h2>
<p class="hmtc-prose" style="font-size:17.5px;line-height:1.72;color:#2b1f1f;margin:0 0 18px">Every limit on every subcategory is the strictest maximum level set by any credible government worldwide, converted to the product&rsquo;s native basis, with a disclosed read-across from the nearest government value where no government regulates the exact product. Nothing is invented; every number traces to a named sovereign limit in the <a href="/regulations" style="color:#1c43ad">regulation references</a>.</p>
<p class="hmtc-prose" style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 4px">Method: <a href="/program/structure" style="color:#1c43ad">government-limit default (Method v2.0)</a>. Pet food is legally feed, not food: its heavy-metal ceilings are set by feed law, EU Directive 2002/32/EC, FDA Center for Veterinary Medicine guidance, and the AAFCO model regulations, whose maximum levels run well above the corresponding human-food limits. HMTc does not certify to that feed floor. Each analyte is set to the strictest applicable human-food sovereign maximum for the nearest human-food analog, adopted as a voluntary human-grade over-compliance; the feed floor is disclosed on every row and never selects the number. Basis is never conflated: dry, as-sold products (kibble, semi-moist, freeze-dried, treats, chews) read across only from dry human analogs, and wet, as-consumed products (canned, raw, toppers, milk replacers, fish-based diets) only from as-consumed analogs, with no wet-to-dry conversion. Tier&nbsp;1 toxics (Pb, Cd, iAs, Hg) are zero-tolerance; Tier&nbsp;2 (tAs, Ni, Cr, Al, Sn) are held to the same government limit; tiering sets the reaction to an exceedance, not any permission to exceed it. Inorganic arsenic and hexavalent chromium operate as reflex speciation screens bounded by total arsenic and total chromium; methylmercury has no terrestrial biomagnification pathway and is bounded by total mercury; tin is material only for canned product, where it migrates from the tin-coated can, and is controlled by screen, not assigned an invented number, on every non-canned form.</p>
