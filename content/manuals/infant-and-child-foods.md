<section style="margin-bottom:52px">
<h2 id="scope" style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">Scope and method</h2>
<p style="font-size:17.5px;line-height:1.72;color:#2b1f1f;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:74ch">Every limit on every subcategory is the strictest maximum level set by any credible government worldwide, converted to the product&rsquo;s native basis, with a disclosed read-across from the nearest government value where no government regulates the exact product. Nothing is invented; every number traces to a named sovereign limit in the <a href="/regulations" style="color:#1c43ad">regulation references</a>.</p>
<p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 4px;max-width:74ch">Method: <a href="/program/structure" style="color:#1c43ad">government-limit default (Method v2.0)</a>. The 8:1 powder-to-liquid basis conversion applies only within infant formula; every other subcategory takes government limits at face value. Tier&nbsp;1 toxics (Pb, Cd, iAs, Hg) are zero-tolerance; Tier&nbsp;2 (tAs, Ni, Al, Sn, Cr) carry the program&rsquo;s 150% transitional allowance. <a href="/analytes/arsenic" style="color:#1c43ad">Arsenic</a> is limited in both forms &mdash; total arsenic (tAs) and inorganic arsenic (iAs) are separate ceilings that must both be met, and the species result never replaces the parent total. Where an analyte is not regulated for a product and is not material in that matrix, it is controlled &mdash; total-<a href="/analytes/chromium" style="color:#1c43ad">chromium</a> screening with reflex hexavalent-chromium speciation, any quantifiable Cr(VI) a hard fail &mdash; rather than assigned an invented number.</p>
</section>
