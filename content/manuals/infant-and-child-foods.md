<p class="hmtc-manual-lead">This Manual is the Method v2 governing instrument for Category 1, Infant and Child Foods. It binds the live subcategory pages; they remain the derivation of each cell.</p>

<section class="hmtc-section">
<h2 id="principles" class="hmtc-h2" style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px">Governing Principles</h2>
<p class="hmtc-cap" style="font-size:13.5px;line-height:1.7;color:#736b6b;margin:0 0 var(--space-3, 24px)">The five objectives are co-equal. None may be sacrificed to advance another. Cited from <span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:12.5px;color:#9f2727">docs/governance/hmtc-governing-principles.md</span> (the ruling named in the readiness manifest).</p><div class="hmtc-principle-grid"><article class="hmtc-principle-card">
<div style="display:flex;align-items:baseline;gap:14px;flex-wrap:wrap;margin-bottom:8px"><span class="hmtc-principle-n">1</span><span class="hmtc-principle-k">Drive down contamination</span></div>
<p>The program exists to lower heavy-metal burden in products that reach people, not to decorate a label.</p>
</article><article class="hmtc-principle-card">
<div style="display:flex;align-items:baseline;gap:14px;flex-wrap:wrap;margin-bottom:8px"><span class="hmtc-principle-n">2</span><span class="hmtc-principle-k">Protect consumers</span></div>
<p>Limits, testing, and the mark are instruments of consumer protection. A certificate that cannot be trusted does not protect anyone.</p>
</article><article class="hmtc-principle-card">
<div style="display:flex;align-items:baseline;gap:14px;flex-wrap:wrap;margin-bottom:8px"><span class="hmtc-principle-n">3</span><span class="hmtc-principle-k">Protect brands who enter</span></div>
<p>A brand that enters in good faith, tests honestly, and stays inside the published rules is not set up to fail. Certification is offered only when the program has high confidence the brand can remain in the program.</p>
</article><article class="hmtc-principle-card">
<div style="display:flex;align-items:baseline;gap:14px;flex-wrap:wrap;margin-bottom:8px"><span class="hmtc-principle-n">4</span><span class="hmtc-principle-k">Remain legally defensible</span></div>
<p>Every published number traces to a named sovereign government maximum or a disclosed read-across. Nothing is invented. Incomplete claim-surface cells are not cert-ready.</p>
</article><article class="hmtc-principle-card">
<div style="display:flex;align-items:baseline;gap:14px;flex-wrap:wrap;margin-bottom:8px"><span class="hmtc-principle-n">5</span><span class="hmtc-principle-k">Become the de facto standard</span></div>
<p>The program is written to be the instrument the market, retailers, and regulators can actually use.</p>
</article></div><div style="border-left:3px solid #9f2727;background:#fdf4f3;padding:var(--space-3, 24px);margin-top:var(--space-3, 24px)"><p class="hmtc-prose" style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#5a4141;margin:0">Method v2.0 (CLAUDE.md Part 19): every cert-ready cell is a government floor or a disclosed read-across. Species never replace the parent total. Limits do not derive from the Heavy Metal Index; every number traces to a named sovereign instrument. The Index is cited one way, outbound only.</p></div>
</section>

<section class="hmtc-section">
<h2 id="lifecycle" class="hmtc-h2" style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px">Program lifecycle and statuses</h2>
<p class="hmtc-prose" style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px">A SKU enters by baseline testing, is assigned a status, and stays under surveillance for as long as the mark is used. Status attaches to the product, not the brand. A product is Certified only when every one of the eight limit analytes is at or below 100% of its published limit — the government floor or disclosed read-across for that cell — confirmed under the measurement-uncertainty rule. There is no band above the limit that still earns the mark.</p><p class="hmtc-cap" style="font-size:13.5px;line-height:1.7;color:#736b6b;margin:0 0 var(--space-3, 24px)">Certified is the only status that carries the mark. A lot in violation of a government legal limit at entry is a hard stop; a lot above any published HMTc limit is not certified and enters Remediation.</p><div class="hmtc-table-wrap"><table style="width:100%;border-collapse:collapse;min-width:36rem"><thead><tr><th style="text-align:left">Status</th><th style="text-align:left">Meaning</th><th style="text-align:left">Mark</th><th style="text-align:left">Condition</th></tr></thead><tbody>
<tr><td>Certified</td><td>Full compliance</td><td>permitted</td><td>every analyte at or below 100% of its published limit</td></tr>
<tr><td>Remediation</td><td>Not certified — applicant or corrective</td><td>withheld / removed</td><td>any analyte above its published limit, or a new applicant not yet at 100%; corrective action and intensified surveillance</td></tr>
<tr><td>Suspension</td><td>Certification halted — hard stop</td><td>removed</td><td>a government legal-limit violation, a data-integrity failure, or failure to execute directives</td></tr>
<tr><td>Revocation</td><td>Permanent termination</td><td>removed</td><td>willful misconduct, data fraud, concealment, or persistent unremediated suspension</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div><p class="hmtc-cap" style="font-size:13.5px;line-height:1.7;color:#736b6b;margin:0 0 var(--space-3, 24px)">Tiering sets how hard and fast the program reacts to an exceedance, never permission to exceed a limit and keep the mark. Tier 1 (Pb, Cd, iAs, mercury as tHg) is zero-tolerance — the most severe reaction. Tier 2 (Ni, Al, Sn, Cr) still bars the mark and enters Remediation, at an elevated rather than immediate hard-stop reaction. Any quantifiable Cr(VI) on a credible pathway is a hard fail, treated as Tier 1. No metal has a transitional band or an allowed overage.</p><div style="border:1px solid #e8e1db;background:#fffdfb;padding:8px 18px 4px;margin:0 0 20px"><div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:minmax(0,1fr) auto;gap:16px;align-items:baseline;padding:10px 0;border-bottom:1px dotted #f2ece7"><span style="font-size:13px;line-height:1.5;color:#3f3a3a">Baseline before any status</span><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:12.5px;color:#9f2727;text-align:right">3 lots, worst-performing lot</span></div><div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:minmax(0,1fr) auto;gap:16px;align-items:baseline;padding:10px 0;border-bottom:1px dotted #f2ece7"><span style="font-size:13px;line-height:1.5;color:#3f3a3a">Prior accredited data</span><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:12.5px;color:#9f2727;text-align:right">credits up to 2 lots</span></div><div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:minmax(0,1fr) auto;gap:16px;align-items:baseline;padding:10px 0;border-bottom:1px dotted #f2ece7"><span style="font-size:13px;line-height:1.5;color:#3f3a3a">Entry hard-stop</span><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:12.5px;color:#9f2727;text-align:right">a government legal-limit violation</span></div><div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:minmax(0,1fr) auto;gap:16px;align-items:baseline;padding:10px 0;border-bottom:1px dotted #f2ece7"><span style="font-size:13px;line-height:1.5;color:#3f3a3a">Suspension reinstatement</span><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:12.5px;color:#9f2727;text-align:right">does not require a fresh baseline</span></div></div>
</section>

<section class="hmtc-section">
<h2 id="instruments" class="hmtc-h2" style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px">Mark, measurement uncertainty, CAPA, remediation, ratchet</h2>
<h3 id="mark" style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:20px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f;margin:28px 0 12px">The mark</h3><p class="hmtc-prose" style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px">The mark may be used only on Certified product — every analyte at or below 100% of its published limit. A product in Remediation carries no mark, at any margin and on any metal, and is not listed as certified while any analyte is above its limit. Suspension and Revocation remove the mark. Claims of heavy-metal free, no toxic metals, or 100% safe are prohibited. The mark certifies testing against published limits under ongoing surveillance; it does not certify safety.</p><h3 id="mu" style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:20px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f;margin:28px 0 12px">Measurement uncertainty</h3><p class="hmtc-prose" style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px">Every result is read with the laboratory's expanded measurement uncertainty at coverage factor k = 2 (95%). Pass only when (result + MU) ≤ limit. Fail when (result − MU) > limit. Anything between is borderline and requires confirmatory action before status issues. A borderline result alone shall not certify. Testing into compliance is prohibited.</p><h3 id="capa" style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:20px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f;margin:28px 0 12px">CAPA</h3><p class="hmtc-cap" style="font-size:13.5px;line-height:1.7;color:#736b6b;margin:0 0 var(--space-3, 24px)">A Tier 2 exceedance in Remediation has a 12-month default to return to Certified. A CAPA closes only after verification of effectiveness and Operator approval.</p><div class="hmtc-table-wrap"><table style="width:100%;border-collapse:collapse;min-width:36rem"><thead><tr><th style="text-align:left">Reaction</th><th style="text-align:left">Initiate</th><th style="text-align:left">Full plan</th><th style="text-align:left">Containment</th></tr></thead><tbody>
<tr><td>Tier 2 exceedance (Remediation)</td><td>10 business days</td><td>30 calendar days</td><td>as needed</td></tr>
<tr><td>Tier 1 exceedance (Remediation)</td><td>5 business days</td><td>15 business days</td><td>within 48 hours</td></tr>
<tr><td>Suspension (hard stop)</td><td>2 business days</td><td>10 business days</td><td>Immediate (within 48 hours)</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div><h3 id="remediation" style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:20px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f;margin:28px 0 12px">Remediation</h3><p class="hmtc-prose" style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px">The Confidential Remediation Track is a confidential entry-and-support pathway for products not yet at 100% of every published limit. It is expressly not a certified status and displays no mark. After certification, Corrective Action follows the published status rules. Concealment of a known exceedance is grounds for revocation. Confidentiality never delays a recall, never overrides a reporting duty, and never withholds evidence from a regulator who asks.</p><h3 id="ratchet" style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:20px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f;margin:28px 0 12px">Standards ratchet</h3><p class="hmtc-prose" style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px">A published limit tightens only through the Standards Ratchet: a stated trigger, a capped step, and a dated edition. Limits do not loosen because a brand found them inconvenient. The subcategory page, not this manual, is the live derivation of each cell.</p>
</section>

<h2 id="lot-testing" class="hmtc-h2" style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px">Lot Testing Schedule</h2>
<p class="hmtc-prose" style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px">Certification is a standing obligation. Totals are run on every completing lot. Speciation is a pathway reflex, not an extra panel seat.
