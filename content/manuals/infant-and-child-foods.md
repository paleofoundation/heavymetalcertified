<section style="margin-bottom:52px">
<h2 id="principles" style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">Governing Principles</h2>
<p style="font-size:13.5px;line-height:1.7;color:#736b6b;margin:0 0 20px;max-width:84ch">The five objectives are co-equal. None may be sacrificed to advance another. Cited from <span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:12.5px;color:#9f2727">docs/governance/hmtc-governing-principles.md</span> (the ruling named in the readiness manifest).</p><div style="border:1px solid #e8e1db;background:#fffdfb"><div style="padding:17px 19px;border-bottom:1px solid #f2ece7">
<div style="display:flex;align-items:baseline;gap:14px;flex-wrap:wrap;margin-bottom:8px"><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:11px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">1</span><span style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-size:19px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f">Drive down contamination</span></div>
<p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0;max-width:74ch">The program exists to lower heavy-metal burden in products that reach people, not to decorate a label.</p>
</div><div style="padding:17px 19px;border-bottom:1px solid #f2ece7">
<div style="display:flex;align-items:baseline;gap:14px;flex-wrap:wrap;margin-bottom:8px"><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:11px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">2</span><span style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-size:19px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f">Protect consumers</span></div>
<p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0;max-width:74ch">Limits, testing, and the mark are instruments of consumer protection. A certificate that cannot be trusted does not protect anyone.</p>
</div><div style="padding:17px 19px;border-bottom:1px solid #f2ece7">
<div style="display:flex;align-items:baseline;gap:14px;flex-wrap:wrap;margin-bottom:8px"><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:11px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">3</span><span style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-size:19px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f">Protect brands who enter</span></div>
<p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0;max-width:74ch">A brand that enters in good faith, tests honestly, and stays inside the published rules is not set up to fail. Certification is offered only when the program has high confidence the brand can remain in the program.</p>
</div><div style="padding:17px 19px;border-bottom:1px solid #f2ece7">
<div style="display:flex;align-items:baseline;gap:14px;flex-wrap:wrap;margin-bottom:8px"><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:11px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">4</span><span style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-size:19px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f">Remain legally defensible</span></div>
<p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0;max-width:74ch">Every published number traces to a named sovereign government maximum or a disclosed read-across. Nothing is invented. Incomplete claim-surface cells are not cert-ready.</p>
</div><div style="padding:17px 19px;border-bottom:1px solid #f2ece7">
<div style="display:flex;align-items:baseline;gap:14px;flex-wrap:wrap;margin-bottom:8px"><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:11px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">5</span><span style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-size:19px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f">Become the de facto standard</span></div>
<p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0;max-width:74ch">The program is written to be the instrument the market, retailers, and regulators can actually use.</p>
</div></div><div style="border-left:3px solid #9f2727;background:#fdf4f3;padding:18px 22px;margin-top:20px"><p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#5a4141;margin:0;max-width:74ch">Method v2.0 (CLAUDE.md Part 19): every cert-ready cell is a government floor or a disclosed read-across. Species never replace the parent total. Limits do not derive from the Heavy Metal Index; every number traces to a named sovereign instrument. The Index is cited one way, outbound only.</p></div>
</section>

<h2 id="lifecycle" style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">Program lifecycle and statuses</h2>
<p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:74ch">A SKU enters by baseline testing, is assigned a status from MaxPoL, and stays under surveillance for as long as the mark is used. Status attaches to the product, not the brand. MaxPoL is the highest percent-of-limit among the eight limit analytes in a lot. The applicable limit is the published government floor or disclosed read-across for that cell.</p><p style="font-size:13.5px;line-height:1.7;color:#736b6b;margin:0 0 20px;max-width:84ch">Status bands are integer and non-overlapping. A lot above 200% MaxPoL, or in violation of a legal limit at entry, is Not Eligible.</p><div style="overflow-x:auto;border:1px solid #e8e1db"><table style="width:100%;border-collapse:collapse;min-width:36rem"><thead><tr><th style="text-align:left">Status</th><th style="text-align:left">Meaning</th><th style="text-align:left">Mark</th><th style="text-align:left">Trigger</th></tr></thead><tbody>
<tr><td>A</td><td>Certified, full compliance</td><td>permitted</td><td>MaxPoL ≤ 100% on every analyte</td></tr>
<tr><td>B</td><td>Transitional</td><td>withheld until A</td><td>101–150%, Tier 2 only, no Tier 1 above 100%</td></tr>
<tr><td>C</td><td>Probation (Low 151–175% / High 176–200%)</td><td>not on new production</td><td>151–200%, or any Tier 1 above 100%, or unmet transitional conditions</td></tr>
<tr><td>D</td><td>Suspension</td><td>prohibited; mark removed</td><td>Above 200% MaxPoL, or a hard-stop</td></tr>
<tr><td>E</td><td>Revocation</td><td>permanently prohibited</td><td>Persistent suspension, willful misconduct, or concealment</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div><p style="font-size:13.5px;line-height:1.7;color:#736b6b;margin:0 0 20px;max-width:84ch">Tier 1 (Pb, Cd, iAs, mercury as tHg) is zero-tolerance. Tier 2 (Ni, Al, Sn, Cr) may use the 150% transitional band under Status B. Any quantifiable Cr(VI) on a credible pathway is a hard fail, treated as Tier 1.</p><div style="border:1px solid #e8e1db;background:#fffdfb;padding:8px 18px 4px;margin:0 0 20px"><div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:minmax(0,1fr) auto;gap:16px;align-items:baseline;padding:10px 0;border-bottom:1px dotted #f2ece7"><span style="font-size:13px;line-height:1.5;color:#3f3a3a">Baseline before any status</span><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:12.5px;color:#9f2727;text-align:right">3 lots, worst-performing lot</span></div><div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:minmax(0,1fr) auto;gap:16px;align-items:baseline;padding:10px 0;border-bottom:1px dotted #f2ece7"><span style="font-size:13px;line-height:1.5;color:#3f3a3a">Prior accredited data</span><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:12.5px;color:#9f2727;text-align:right">credits up to 2 lots</span></div><div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:minmax(0,1fr) auto;gap:16px;align-items:baseline;padding:10px 0;border-bottom:1px dotted #f2ece7"><span style="font-size:13px;line-height:1.5;color:#3f3a3a">Entry hard-stop</span><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:12.5px;color:#9f2727;text-align:right">above 200% MaxPoL, or a legal-limit violation</span></div><div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:minmax(0,1fr) auto;gap:16px;align-items:baseline;padding:10px 0;border-bottom:1px dotted #f2ece7"><span style="font-size:13px;line-height:1.5;color:#3f3a3a">Status D reinstatement</span><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:12.5px;color:#9f2727;text-align:right">does not require a fresh baseline</span></div></div>
</section>

<section style="margin-bottom:52px">
<h2 id="instruments" style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">Mark, measurement uncertainty, CAPA, remediation, ratchet</h2>
<h3 id="mark" style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:20px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f;margin:28px 0 12px">The mark</h3><p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:74ch">The mark may be used only on Status A product. Status B is tracked as transitional and does not display the mark. Status C may release a lot only when that lot itself tests at Status A. Status D and E prohibit the mark. Claims of heavy-metal free, no toxic metals, or 100% safe are prohibited. The mark certifies testing against published limits under ongoing surveillance; it does not certify safety.</p><h3 id="mu" style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:20px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f;margin:28px 0 12px">Measurement uncertainty</h3><p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:74ch">Every result is read with the laboratory's expanded measurement uncertainty at coverage factor k = 2 (95%). Pass only when (result + MU) ≤ limit. Fail when (result − MU) > limit. Anything between is borderline and requires confirmatory action before status issues. A borderline result alone shall not certify. Testing into compliance is prohibited.</p><h3 id="capa" style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:20px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f;margin:28px 0 12px">CAPA</h3><p style="font-size:13.5px;line-height:1.7;color:#736b6b;margin:0 0 20px;max-width:84ch">Status B has a 12-month default to return to Status A. A CAPA closes only after verification of effectiveness and Operator approval.</p><div style="overflow-x:auto;border:1px solid #e8e1db"><table style="width:100%;border-collapse:collapse;min-width:36rem"><thead><tr><th style="text-align:left">Status</th><th style="text-align:left">Initiate</th><th style="text-align:left">Full plan</th><th style="text-align:left">Containment</th></tr></thead><tbody>
<tr><td>B</td><td>10 business days</td><td>30 calendar days</td><td>as needed</td></tr>
<tr><td>C</td><td>5 business days</td><td>15 business days</td><td>within 48 hours</td></tr>
<tr><td>D</td><td>2 business days</td><td>10 business days</td><td>Immediate (within 48 hours)</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div><h3 id="remediation" style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:20px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f;margin:28px 0 12px">Remediation</h3><p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:74ch">Before certification, the Readiness Pathway is nonpublic and awards no mark. After certification, Corrective Action follows the published status rules. Concealment of a known exceedance is grounds for revocation. Confidentiality never delays a recall, never overrides a reporting duty, and never withholds evidence from a regulator who asks.</p><h3 id="ratchet" style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:20px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f;margin:28px 0 12px">Standards ratchet</h3><p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:74ch">A published limit tightens only through the Standards Ratchet: a stated trigger, a capped step, and a dated edition. Limits do not loosen because a brand found them inconvenient. The subcategory page, not this manual, is the live derivation of each cell.</p>
</section>

<h2 id="lot-testing" style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">Lot Testing Schedule</h2>
<p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:74ch">Certification is a standing obligation. Totals are run on every completing lot.
