<section class="hmtc-section">
<h2 id="scope" class="hmtc-h2" style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px">Scope and method</h2>
<p class="hmtc-prose" style="font-size:17.5px;line-height:1.72;color:#2b1f1f;margin:0 0 18px">Every limit on every subcategory is the strictest maximum level set by any credible government worldwide, converted to the product&rsquo;s native basis, with a disclosed read-across from the nearest government value where no government regulates the exact product. Nothing is invented; every number traces to a named sovereign limit in the <a href="/regulations" style="color:#1c43ad">regulation references</a>.</p>
<p class="hmtc-prose" style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 4px">Method: <a href="/program/structure" style="color:#1c43ad">government-limit default (Method v2.0)</a>. Seafood limits are taken at face value in each product&rsquo;s native basis; there is no reconstitution conversion. Mercury is limited in both forms, total mercury (Hg) and methylmercury (MeHg), set to the species-specific tier each government assigns (predatory fish carry the higher ceiling, lower-trophic species the lower; where the total-mercury ceiling is stricter, methylmercury is bounded by it). Tier&nbsp;1 toxics (Pb, Cd, iAs, Hg) are zero-tolerance; Tier&nbsp;2 (tAs, Ni, Cr, Cr-VI, Sn, Al) are held to the same government limit; tiering sets the reaction to an exceedance, not any permission to exceed it. Total arsenic operates as a reflex screen to inorganic-arsenic speciation. Every panel analyte carries a limit of record; where no government regulates the exact product (nickel, hexavalent chromium, aluminium, and non-canned tin), the ceiling is a disclosed read-across to the nearest applicable sovereign instrument named in the derivation. Tin migrates from the can, so for non-canned seafood the read-across is a packaging-migration baseline.</p>
</section>
