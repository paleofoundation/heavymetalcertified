<section style="margin-bottom:52px">
<h2 id="scope" style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">Scope and method</h2>
<p style="font-size:17.5px;line-height:1.72;color:#2b1f1f;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:74ch">Every limit on every subcategory is the strictest maximum level set by any credible government worldwide, converted to the product&rsquo;s native basis, with a disclosed read-across from the nearest government value where no government regulates the exact product. Nothing is invented; every number traces to a named sovereign limit in the <a href="/regulations" style="color:#1c43ad">regulation references</a>.</p>
<p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 4px;max-width:74ch">Method: <a href="/program/structure" style="color:#1c43ad">government-limit default (Method v2.0)</a>. Dairy limits are taken at face value in each product's native basis; there is no reconstitution conversion (dairy has no legislated reconstitution ratio, unlike infant formula). Product forms — liquid milk, cheese, powder, condensed, butterfat — are kept distinct: non-concentrated liquid forms share the general fluid-milk sovereign pool, while concentrated forms carry their own higher form-specific government maximums. Tier 1 toxics (Pb, Cd, iAs, Hg) are zero-tolerance; Tier 2 (tAs, Ni, Sn, Al, Cr, Cr-VI) carry the program's 150% transitional allowance. Inorganic arsenic and hexavalent chromium are reflex speciation screens; methylmercury has no pathway in dairy; tin is material only for canned product.</p>
</section>

What the pooled occurrence literature shows is where the work is &mdash; which ingredients demand exceptional sourcing.</p><div class="hmtc-mhard-list"><details class="hmtc-mhard" open>

No government sets an aluminium limit for solid infant food, so the standard holds these products to the strictest sovereign infant aluminium ceiling &mdash; the AU-NZ infant-formula limit &mdash; as a protective read-across, rather than to the only limit that names solid foods (an adult additive-residue allowance many times looser).
