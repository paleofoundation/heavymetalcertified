<section class="hmtc-section">
<h2 id="scope" class="hmtc-h2" style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px">Scope and method</h2>
<p class="hmtc-prose" style="font-size:17.5px;line-height:1.72;color:#2b1f1f;margin:0 0 18px">This manual states how Category 25 Cannabis, Hemp, and CBD Products is certified for metal safety. It does not certify efficacy, legality, or approval status. Inorganic arsenic is capped at total arsenic and tested on every lot. It does not carry a separate number, and it never replaces the parent. Methylmercury is capped at total mercury and tested on every lot. It does not carry a separate number, and it never replaces the parent. Hexavalent chromium is capped at total chromium and tested on every lot. It does not carry a separate number, and it never replaces the parent. Every figure in the matrix is read from the live standard record. Nothing in this manual invents a ceiling.</p>
<p class="hmtc-prose" style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 4px">Method: <a href="/program/structure" style="color:#1c43ad">government-limit default (Method v2.0)</a>. Why Method v1 was retired is on <a href="/program/method" style="color:#1c43ad">the method page</a>. The six live standards remain the derivation of each cell. A product is certified only at or below 100% of every published ceiling. The v1 PDF carries the program rules and the standards register, including each house DOI.</p>
</section>

<p class="hmtc-prose" style="font-size:13.5px;line-height:1.7;color:#736b6b;margin:0 0 20px">All values are ppb (&micro;g/kg), in the row's basis. Click any subcategory for its derivation. It is not an empty cell and not an invented number.</p>
