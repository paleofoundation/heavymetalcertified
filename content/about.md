<div style="border-left:3px solid #9f2727;background:#fdf4f3;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:52px">
  <div style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:9.5px;letter-spacing:.14em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#9f2727;margin-bottom:10px">For journalists</div>
  <p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#5a4141;margin:0">Heavy Metal Tested &amp; Certified (HMTc) is a voluntary third-party certification program operated and governed by the Institute of Contaminant Standards (ICS), a trade name of Paleo Certified, Inc. (Paleo Foundation context). HMTc publishes subcategory numeric heavy-metal limits and certifies products that independent ISO/IEC 17025-accredited laboratories find at or below those published values, under ongoing lot surveillance. The mark is per product and must appear with a scope line naming the standard and edition. Certification measures conformity to a published threshold; it is not a health verdict. Canonical site: https://heavymetalcertified.com/</p>
</div>

<section id="what-it-is" style="margin-bottom:52px">
  <h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">What HMTc is</h2>
  <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">Heavy Metal Tested &amp; Certified (HMTc) is a voluntary third-party certification program that publishes numeric heavy-metal limits by product subcategory and certifies products against those published limits. Testing is performed by independent laboratories accredited to ISO/IEC 17025. The program is operated and governed by the Institute of Contaminant Standards (ICS). Certification is a statement about measurement against a published threshold, not a health verdict on a product or diet.</p>
  <div style="border:1px solid #e8e1db;background:#fffdfb;margin-bottom:20px">
    <div style="padding:16px 19px;border-bottom:1px solid #f2ece7"><p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0">Voluntary third-party certification against published subcategory numeric limits.</p></div>
    <div style="padding:16px 19px;border-bottom:1px solid #f2ece7"><p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0">Limits are per product subcategory, in the product's native basis, with the edition and year shown on the live standard.</p></div>
    <div style="padding:16px 19px;border-bottom:1px solid #f2ece7"><p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0">Products earn the mark via independent ISO/IEC 17025-accredited laboratory testing at or below every published value for that subcategory, under ongoing lot surveillance.</p></div>
    <div style="padding:16px 19px;border-bottom:1px solid #f2ece7"><p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0">The mark is per product, not brand-wide, and must appear with a scope line naming the standard and edition.</p></div>
    <div style="padding:16px 19px;border-bottom:1px solid #f2ece7"><p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0">Operated and governed by the Institute of Contaminant Standards (ICS).</p></div>
