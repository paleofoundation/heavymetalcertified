## Make the proof useful to a buyer

The proposed Challenge connects authenticated lab evidence, real inventory and buyer demand. Its purpose is to turn proof into work that a brand, supplier or retailer can check and use.

For brands, that begins with finished-product evidence. For suppliers, it begins with a bounded ingredient lot. [TECRID](https://tecrid.com) makes the lab-result record checkable. [VLE](https://vle.exchange) evaluates a bounded lot against its frozen Compliance Profile. [HMTc](/standards) evaluates finished goods against the applicable published numbers.
