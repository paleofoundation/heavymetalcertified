<section id="standard-setting" style="margin-bottom:52px">
  <h2>Standard-setting</h2>
  <p>The method begins with the product form. Each subcategory is assessed in its native basis, against the strictest applicable government maximum and the admitted occurrence record. A value publishes only after its evidence, conversion, coverage, method and governance checks are complete.</p>

<section id="measures" style="margin-bottom:52px">
  <h2>What the program measures</h2>
  <p>The Big 8 are lead, cadmium, arsenic, mercury, nickel, tin, aluminum and chromium. Arsenic and mercury are also resolved by species where the standard requires it, producing a ten-analyte reporting panel.</p>

<section id="evidence-base">
  <h2>Evidence and policy</h2>
  <p>Heavy Metal Certified publishes the standards and certification record. The independent Heavy Metal Index holds the literature layer that supports the occurrence and toxicology record. Regulatory instruments remain identifiable as law or guidance; they are never presented as HMTc thresholds.</p>
