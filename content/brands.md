The directory lists every brand carrying the Heavy Metal Tested & Certified mark, with the route to check any single certificate. Listing is optional — the directory is a published list, not a census.

No certificates are in force yet. When the register opens, this page becomes the public directory; until then, it describes how the directory will work.

## Check a single certificate

Use [Verify a certificate](/verify) to check a lot number, certificate number, or product code against the register.

## For retailers

The scope of the mark, the certification statuses, and what a certificate asserts are described in the [program structure](/program/structure). Brands seeking certification start at [Apply](/apply).
