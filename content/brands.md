No product certificates have been issued, so this directory is empty.

This is an optional discovery surface, not the authoritative census of certificates. When a participating brand chooses to be listed, its entry must name the exact certified product and form and link to the product-specific verification record.

## Directory and verification are different

| Surface | Purpose | What absence means |
|---|---|---|
| Certified Products Directory | Lets a brand feature selected certified products in a browsable public list. | Nothing by itself. The governing manual permits optional directory visibility. |
| Certificate lookup | Resolves a certificate number, product code, or lot identifier to the authoritative product-specific record. | An identifier presented with the mark that does not resolve, mismatches the product, or shows an inactive status requires investigation. |

A brand with one certified product is not a “certified brand.” It is a brand with one product certified within a named form, standard, edition, status, and time period.

## What a directory entry will contain

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| Brand and legal holder | Identifies who holds the product authorization. |
| Product and form | Prevents one SKU or basis from implying portfolio-wide scope. |
| Certificate and status | Links to the current verification record. |
| Standard and edition | Identifies the rule governing the decision. |
| Authorized scope line | States the eight-metal, ten-analyte testing claim without implying safety or zero metals. |
| Brand-supplied description | Visually and textually separated from the certification record. |

## Reporting suspected misuse

Do not treat absence from this optional directory as proof of misuse. First use the certificate or product code printed with the claim.

Report a mark when the identifier does not resolve, the product or form does not match, the record is expired, suspended, or revoked, the artwork has been altered, or the surrounding claim implies safety, zero metals, or broader certification than the record supports.
