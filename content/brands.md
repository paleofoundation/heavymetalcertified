<section id="scope">
  <h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">Reading the mark</h2>
  <div style="border-left:3px solid #9f2727;background:#fdf4f3;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:20px">
    <p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#5a4141;margin:0">The mark on a pack means that product was tested at or below every value in the standard named for its subcategory, in that product&rsquo;s native basis, on the stated analyte panel. It does not mean the product is safe, that the brand&rsquo;s other products are certified, or that analytes outside the panel were controlled. Scope travels with the mark, and it is stated here rather than left to inference.</p>
  </div>
  <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">A brand appearing in this register with one certified product is not a certified brand. It is a brand with a certified product, and the page says which one.</p>
</section>
