<section id="why" style="margin-bottom:52px">
  <h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">Why it exists</h2>
  <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">A brand whose product is not yet certifiable has two bad options. It can ship uncertified and say nothing, or it can expose an early, unfinished result and hand a plaintiff a factual predicate before any correction has been made. Either way the rational move becomes to test less, test fewer lots, and avoid asking questions whose answers create disclosure obligations. A program built only around a pass or a public fail gets less discovery, not more.</p>
  <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">The Track is the third option. A product that does not yet reach Status&nbsp;A or&nbsp;B &mdash; but whose every lot still meets its applicable legal limits &mdash; enters a defined, confidential improvement process. The corrective action is real, the re-testing is real, and the mark is withheld until the product actually qualifies. What the brand gets is not a certificate. It is a documented, timestamped, third-party-verified record of improvement that exists before any inquiry.</p>
  <div style="border-left:3px solid #9f2727;background:#fdf4f3;padding:18px 22px">
    <p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#5a4141;margin:0">The asymmetry is deliberate and it is the whole mechanism: structured correction is cheaper than concealment. Concealment of a known exceedance is grounds for revocation, the most severe outcome the program has.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section id="what" style="margin-bottom:52px">
  <h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">What the Track is</h2>
  <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">An entry and support pathway for brands whose initial baseline does not reach Status&nbsp;A or&nbsp;B, provided every tested lot meets the applicable legal heavy-metal limits in its markets of sale at the time of testing. It is a route into the program for products not yet certifiable &mdash; not a post-certification mechanism, and not a way for an already-certified product to stay certified through a finding.</p>
  <div style="border:1px solid #e8e1db;background:#fffdfb;padding:16px 18px;margin-bottom:16px">
    <div style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:9.5px;letter-spacing:.14em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#9f2727;margin-bottom:8px">What enrolment carries</div>
    <div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:minmax(0,1fr) auto;gap:16px;align-items:baseline;padding:10px 0;border-bottom:1px dotted #f2ece7"><span style="font-size:13px;line-height:1.5;color:#3f3a3a">Stage</span><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:12.5px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">pre-certification</span></div>
    <div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:minmax(0,1fr) auto;gap:16px;align-items:baseline;padding:10px 0;border-bottom:1px dotted #f2ece7"><span style="font-size:13px;line-height:1.5;color:#3f3a3a">Certificate issued</span><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:12.5px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">none</span></div>
    <div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:minmax(0,1fr) auto;gap:16px;align-items:baseline;padding:10px 0;border-bottom:1px dotted #f2ece7"><span style="font-size:13px;line-height:1.5;color:#3f3a3a">Mark authorised</span><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:12.5px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">not until Status A or B</span></div>
    <div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:minmax(0,1fr) auto;gap:16px;align-items:baseline;padding:10px 0;border-bottom:1px dotted #f2ece7"><span style="font-size:13px;line-height:1.5;color:#3f3a3a">Confidentiality</span><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:12.5px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">enrolment, test data, CAPA</span></div>
    <div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:minmax(0,1fr) auto;gap:16px;align-items:baseline;padding:10px 0;border-bottom:1px dotted #f2ece7"><span style="font-size:13px;line-height:1.5;color:#3f3a3a">Support</span><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:12.5px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">reformulation, supplier intelligence</span></div>
    <div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:minmax(0,1fr) auto;gap:16px;align-items:baseline;padding:10px 0;border-bottom:1px dotted #f2ece7"><span style="font-size:13px;line-height:1.5;color:#3f3a3a">Corrective-action windows</span><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:12.5px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">standard CAPA (Part 2.8)</span></div>
    <div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:minmax(0,1fr) auto;gap:16px;align-items:baseline;padding:10px 0"><span style="font-size:13px;line-height:1.5;color:#3f3a3a">With prior-data credit</span><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:12.5px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">not available together</span></div>
  </div>
  <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0;max-width:72ch">The mark-use rule is hard. No product, packaging, or marketing may carry the Certification Mark until at least one SKU reaches Status&nbsp;A or&nbsp;B. The Track protects the record, not the right to advertise.</p>
</section>

  <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">The Track is for a baseline that falls short of certification while every lot still clears the law. <section id="process" style="margin-bottom:52px">
  <h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">How enrolment runs</h2>
  <div style="border:1px solid #e8e1db;background:#fffdfb;margin-bottom:16px">
    <div style="padding:16px 19px;border-bottom:1px solid #f2ece7"><div style="display:flex;align-items:baseline;gap:14px;flex-wrap:wrap;margin-bottom:7px"><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:11px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">01</span><span style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-size:18px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f">Enter</span><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:10px;letter-spacing:.06em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#a9a1a1;margin-left:auto;white-space:nowrap">day 0</span></div><p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0;max-width:74ch">The brand enrols the SKU, naming what the baseline shows, the markets of sale, and a proposed direction. Confidentiality applies from first contact, not from the time an account exists.</p></div>
    <div style="padding:16px 19px;border-bottom:1px solid #f2ece7"><div style="display:flex;align-items:baseline;gap:14px;flex-wrap:wrap;margin-bottom:7px"><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:11px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">02</span><span style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-size:18px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f">Scope</span><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:10px;letter-spacing:.06em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#a9a1a1;margin-left:auto;white-space:nowrap">days 1&ndash;10</span></div><p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0;max-width:74ch">The program and the brand agree what is driving the result: which analytes, which inputs, and whether reflex or speciated testing is needed for the matrix.</p></div>
    <div style="padding:16px 19px;border-bottom:1px solid #f2ece7"><div style="display:flex;align-items:baseline;gap:14px;flex-wrap:wrap;margin-bottom:7px"><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:11px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">03</span><span style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-size:18px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f">Corrective action</span><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:10px;letter-spacing:.06em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#a9a1a1;margin-left:auto;white-space:nowrap">standard CAPA</span></div><p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0;max-width:74ch">Reformulation, supplier requalification, or a process change &mdash; whatever the root cause requires, on the standard governance CAPA windows in Part&nbsp;2.8, with a dated deadline recorded against the item.</p></div>
    <div style="padding:16px 19px;border-bottom:1px solid #f2ece7"><div style="display:flex;align-items:baseline;gap:14px;flex-wrap:wrap;margin-bottom:7px"><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:11px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">04</span><span style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-size:18px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f">Re-test</span><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:10px;letter-spacing:.06em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#a9a1a1;margin-left:auto;white-space:nowrap">after action</span></div><p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0;max-width:74ch">Fresh lots run the full analyte panel at an accredited laboratory &mdash; enough lots to show the baseline has actually moved, not one favorable result.</p></div>
    <div style="padding:16px 19px"><div style="display:flex;align-items:baseline;gap:14px;flex-wrap:wrap;margin-bottom:7px"><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:11px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">05</span><span style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-size:18px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f">Certify or continue</span><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:10px;letter-spacing:.06em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#a9a1a1;margin-left:auto;white-space:nowrap">on evidence</span></div><p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0;max-width:74ch">When a SKU meets Status&nbsp;A or&nbsp;B it leaves the Track and is certified, and the mark becomes available for that SKU. Until then the mark stays withheld and the record stays confidential.</p></div>
  </div>
  <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0;max-width:72ch">A regulatory exceedance at any point is a hard stop that takes absolute precedence over program status: it triggers licensee notification, a mandatory hold or market correction, and program cooperation with regulators, regardless of enrolment.</p>
</section>

<section id="confidentiality" style="margin-bottom:52px">

  <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">Confidentiality here is a commercial protection, not a shield against a safety obligation. Stating its boundaries plainly is what keeps the rest of it credible.</p>

Enrolment and its documentation are produced to a regulator on request without a subpoena, and a product that should not be on shelf comes off shelf.</p>

<section id="open">
  <h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">Entering the Track</h2>
  <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">Enrolment is part of an application. A brand names the SKU it wants to move toward certification and the markets it sells in; the confidentiality applies from first contact, not from the time an account exists. A brand already in the application process adds a SKU to the Track from within it.</p>
  <div style="border:1px solid #f2dcda;background:#fdf4f3;padding:24px 26px">
    <div style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-size:24px;line-height:1.4;color:#2b1f1f;margin-bottom:10px">Enrol a not-yet-certifiable product</div>
    <p style="font-size:15px;line-height:1.72;color:#5a4141;margin:0 0 16px;max-width:70ch">You will be asked which product you want to bring toward certification, what its current baseline shows, and where it is sold. No certificate is issued and no mark is authorised while a product is on the Track; what it builds is a confidential, dated record of the correction.</p>
