<section id="why" style="margin-bottom:52px">
  <h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">Why it exists</h2>
  <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">Every certification program faces the same problem. The moment a finding becomes a public event, the rational move for a brand is to stop looking &mdash; test less often, test fewer lots, avoid asking questions whose answers create disclosure obligations. A program that punishes discovery gets less discovery, and the mark it issues becomes a statement about how little a brand knows.</p>
  <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">This track is the answer to that. A brand that identifies a problem and reports it enters a defined, confidential process with its certificate intact. The corrective action is not reduced, the re-testing is not reduced, and the deadline is real &mdash; but the finding does not become a press cycle for having been honest.</p>
  <div style="border-left:3px solid #9f2727;background:#fdf4f3;padding:18px 22px">
    <p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#5a4141;margin:0">The asymmetry is deliberate and it is the whole mechanism: self-reporting is cheaper than concealment. Concealment of a known exceedance is grounds for revocation, which is the most severe outcome the program has.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section id="pathways" style="margin-bottom:52px">

  <p style="font-size:13.5px;line-height:1.7;color:#736b6b;margin:0 0 20px;max-width:84ch">Same corrective action, same re-testing, same deadline. What differs is disclosure &mdash; and each direction carries a real benefit, which is why it is a choice rather than a default.</p>

      <p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 14px">The finding, the affected lots and the corrective action stay between the brand and the program while the item is open and on schedule. Nothing appears in the public register, nothing is disclosed to retailers, and the certificate is unaffected.</p>

      <p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 14px">The brand elects to publish the finding and its remediation on its own brand page. The record shows what was found, what was done and when it closed &mdash; a documented history of catching and fixing a problem rather than an unexplained gap.</p>

      <div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:minmax(0,1fr) auto;gap:16px;align-items:baseline;padding:10px 0;border-bottom:1px dotted #f2ece7"><span style="font-size:13px;line-height:1.5;color:#3f3a3a">Citable by the brand</span><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:12.5px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">yes</span></div>

  <p style="font-size:11.5px;line-height:1.55;color:#a9a1a1;max-width:84ch">A brand may move an item from confidential to public at any time. It cannot move one the other way once disclosed, because the disclosure has already happened.</p>

<section id="process" style="margin-bottom:52px">
  <h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">How an item runs</h2>
  <div style="border:1px solid #e8e1db;background:#fffdfb;margin-bottom:16px">
    <div style="padding:16px 19px;border-bottom:1px solid #f2ece7"><div style="display:flex;align-items:baseline;gap:14px;flex-wrap:wrap;margin-bottom:7px"><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:11px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">01</span><span style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-size:18px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f">Report</span><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:10px;letter-spacing:.06em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#a9a1a1;margin-left:auto;white-space:nowrap">day 0</span></div><p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0;max-width:74ch">The brand opens an item naming what was found, the affected products and lots, and a proposed direction. A completed root-cause analysis is not required to open.</p></div>
    <div style="padding:16px 19px;border-bottom:1px solid #f2ece7"><div style="display:flex;align-items:baseline;gap:14px;flex-wrap:wrap;margin-bottom:7px"><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:11px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">02</span><span style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-size:18px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f">Scope</span><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:10px;letter-spacing:.06em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#a9a1a1;margin-left:auto;white-space:nowrap">days 1&ndash;10</span></div><p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0;max-width:74ch">The program and the brand agree what is affected: which SKUs, which lots, whether product on shelf is implicated, and whether reflex or speciated testing is needed.</p></div>
    <div style="padding:16px 19px;border-bottom:1px solid #f2ece7"><div style="display:flex;align-items:baseline;gap:14px;flex-wrap:wrap;margin-bottom:7px"><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:11px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">03</span><span style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-size:18px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f">Corrective action</span><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:10px;letter-spacing:.06em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#a9a1a1;margin-left:auto;white-space:nowrap">agreed window</span></div><p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0;max-width:74ch">The brand executes. Supplier requalification, process change, specification update &mdash; whatever the root cause requires, with a dated deadline recorded against the item.</p></div>
    <div style="padding:16px 19px;border-bottom:1px solid #f2ece7"><div style="display:flex;align-items:baseline;gap:14px;flex-wrap:wrap;margin-bottom:7px"><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:11px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">04</span><span style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-size:18px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f">Re-test</span><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:10px;letter-spacing:.06em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#a9a1a1;margin-left:auto;white-space:nowrap">after action</span></div><p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0;max-width:74ch">Fresh lots run the full analyte panel at an accredited laboratory. A trend item requires enough lots to show the trend has actually turned, not one favorable result.</p></div>
    <div style="padding:16px 19px"><div style="display:flex;align-items:baseline;gap:14px;flex-wrap:wrap;margin-bottom:7px"><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:11px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">05</span><span style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-size:18px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f">Close</span><span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:10px;letter-spacing:.06em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#a9a1a1;margin-left:auto;white-space:nowrap">on evidence</span></div><p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0;max-width:74ch">The item closes against the re-test evidence. Under the confidential track it closes without ever having been public; under the public track the closure is published with it.</p></div>
  </div>
  <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">An item that stays on schedule has no effect on the certificate or the public register. An item that misses its corrective-action deadline converts to the standard status process, and the status change is visible in the register like any other.</p>
</section>

<section id="confidentiality" style="margin-bottom:52px">

  <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">Confidentiality here is a commercial protection, not a shield against a safety obligation. Stating its boundaries plainly is what keeps the rest of it credible.</p>
