A brand that finds a correctable problem before certification should have a controlled route to address it. That route should encourage early investigation without converting a private readiness finding into a public failed-product event.

The current Program Manual defines the Confidential Remediation Track as a pre-certification entry and support pathway. It is available when an initial baseline does not reach a certifiable status, provided the tested product remains within applicable legal heavy-metal limits in its markets of sale. The track creates no certificate and authorizes no use of the mark.

## What the track is

| Attribute | Rule |
|---|---|
| Program stage | Before certification |
| Purpose | Identify and correct the condition preventing certification |
| Public applicant status | None |
| Certificate | Not issued |
| Mark use | Not permitted |
| Corrective action | Required under the applicable plan and governance windows |
| Reassessment | Available after the defined correction and evidence requirements are complete |
| Legal and regulatory duties | Unchanged |

This resolves an important boundary: confidential remediation protects the pre-certification process. It does not create a hidden certified status.

## What qualifies

| Situation | Route |
|---|---|
| Private Readiness identifies an evidence gap but no adverse product signal | Complete the evidence gap; remediation may not be necessary. |
| Initial baseline is not certifiable but all tested lots remain within applicable legal limits | Confidential Remediation may be available. |
| A supplier, process, facility, packaging, or formula change creates a known pre-certification contamination risk | Open the issue early and define the testing and control plan. |
| A result is analytically borderline | Follow the published confirmatory rule before treating it as a remediation case. |
| A result may exceed an applicable legal limit | Stop the ordinary readiness path and activate the brand's quality, regulatory, and legal response. |
| A product is already certified and later develops a nonconformity | Follow the governing certification status, surveillance, CAPA, mark, suspension, revocation, and reinstatement rules. This pre-certification track does not control that event. |
| Data have been concealed, manipulated, selectively omitted, or repeatedly tested without an approved basis | Treat as a data-integrity matter, not confidential remediation. |

## The workflow

| Stage | Required work | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Define | Identify product, lots, analytes, results, applicable limits, and known changes | Controlled issue record |
| 2. Contain | Prevent additional uncertainty and preserve samples, records, suppliers, and lots | Containment record and evidence hold |
| 3. Confirm | Resolve measurement uncertainty, sample identity, method, custody, and result validity under predefined rules | Confirmed analytical state |
| 4. Investigate | Evaluate ingredient contribution, suppliers, origins, water, equipment, facility, process, packaging, season, and formulation | Root-cause hypothesis and test plan |
| 5. Correct | Change source, specification, formulation, process, facility control, packaging, or monitoring as supported by evidence | Corrective and preventive actions |
| 6. Verify | Test the required validation lots and determine whether the action worked | Verification of effectiveness |
| 7. Reassess | Re-enter formal certification only when the product and evidence are ready | New certification decision under the governing standard |

The program should not require a completed root-cause analysis before an issue can be opened. Early reporting is useful precisely because the investigation is not yet complete.

## What remains confidential

Subject to the applicable agreement and law, the following are handled as confidential business information rather than published as a failed certification record:

| Record | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Readiness participation | Not placed in a public applicant list |
| Product and formulation information | Controlled certification file |
| Supplier and origin information | Controlled certification file |
| Laboratory results and supporting metadata | Controlled certification file |
| CAPA, investigation, and remediation progress | Controlled certification file |
| Decision to stop or defer before certification | Not described as a public revocation or suspension because no certificate exists |

Private readiness and remediation data do not enter the public Heavy Metal Index. HMI is built from public and separately governed evidence sources, not applicant files.

## Limits of confidentiality

Contractual confidentiality is not legal privilege and is not a safety shield.

| Confidentiality does not | Rule |
|---|---|
| Delay a recall or market correction | Never |
| Override a reporting duty | Never |
| Defeat a regulator request or compulsory legal process | Never |
| Avoid a legal hold | Never |
| Excuse a contractual notice obligation | Never |
| Authorize continued sale of product that should be held or removed | Never |
| Permit mark use before certification | Never |
| Convert concealment into protected remediation | Never |

The brand remains responsible for obtaining its own legal advice and complying with every applicable duty. The program should preserve records and cooperate as required without representing commercial confidentiality as immunity.

## The post-certification boundary

Once a product is certified, the certificate and mark must continue to describe reality. Later adverse results are governed by the applicable Program Manual and certificate status.

The public site must not promise that a voluntary disclosure automatically leaves a certificate, public record, or mark unaffected. If the Institute chooses to create a distinct post-certification voluntary-disclosure protocol, it must be separately ratified and define eligibility, status, mark use, public verification, retailer communication, CAPA, deadlines, legal duties, and reinstatement. Until then, the existing A–E status framework controls certified-product events.

## The governing principle

Finding a problem early should give a responsible brand a clearer route forward than avoiding the question. That incentive must come from process certainty, fair confirmation, controlled investigation, and a real path to reassessment, not from an unsupported promise that every finding remains private or every certificate remains active.
