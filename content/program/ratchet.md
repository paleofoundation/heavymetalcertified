<section id="two-mechanisms" style="margin-bottom:52px">
  <h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">Two mechanisms, two clocks</h2>
  <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">A published limit is not a fixed number. It is a starting value that tightens on a schedule as the certified market improves. Two separate mechanisms govern a limit across its life, and they run on different data and on different clocks. Confusing them is the most common way to misread the program.</p>
  <div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:repeat(auto-fit,minmax(17rem,1fr));gap:1px;background:#e8e1db;border:1px solid #e8e1db;margin-bottom:16px">
    <div style="background:#fffdfb;padding:19px">
      <div style="display:flex;align-items:baseline;gap:12px;margin-bottom:10px">
        <span style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-size:20px;color:#2b1f1f">Where a limit starts</span>
        <span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:9.5px;letter-spacing:.08em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#9f2727;background:#fdf4f3;border:1px solid #f2dcda;border-radius:2px;padding:3px 8px;white-space:nowrap">At publication</span>
      </div>
      <p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0">The limit is set from the strictest credible government maximum level, floored against the pooled occurrence distribution for the four Tier&nbsp;1 toxics and set from occurrence alone where no government regulates the cell. This uses external data &mdash; agency surveys, total-diet studies, and the peer-reviewed literature. Its job is to anchor the published value to a sovereign standard.</p>
    </div>
    <div style="background:#fffdfb;padding:19px">
      <div style="display:flex;align-items:baseline;gap:12px;margin-bottom:10px">
        <span style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-size:20px;color:#2b1f1f">How a limit tightens</span>
        <span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:9.5px;letter-spacing:.08em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#9f2727;background:#fdf4f3;border:1px solid #f2dcda;border-radius:2px;padding:3px 8px;white-space:nowrap">Each year after</span>
      </div>
      <p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0">The ratchet lowers the published limit as certified brands clean up, measured by the program&rsquo;s own certified-lot results and reviewed annually. This uses internal data &mdash; the lots the program tests &mdash; and it moves without waiting for any regulator to revise a maximum level.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section id="how-it-fires" style="margin-bottom:52px">
  <h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">How the ratchet fires</h2>
  <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">The ratchet for an analyte fires only when the certified market has demonstrably moved, tested two ways so that a single large brand cannot pull a limit down on its own. Both conditions must hold for two consecutive annual reviews before a limit is revised.</p>
  <div style="border:1px solid #e8e1db;background:#fffdfb;margin-bottom:16px">
    <div style="padding:17px 19px;border-bottom:1px solid #f2ece7">
      <div style="display:flex;align-items:baseline;gap:14px;flex-wrap:wrap;margin-bottom:8px">
        <span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:11px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">Condition A</span>
        <span style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-size:19px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f">Aggregate shift</span>
      </div>
      <p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0;max-width:74ch">The 80th percentile of certified Status&nbsp;A lot results sits at or below 80% of the current limit.</p>
    </div>
    <div style="padding:17px 19px;border-bottom:1px solid #f2ece7">
      <div style="display:flex;align-items:baseline;gap:14px;flex-wrap:wrap;margin-bottom:8px">
        <span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:11px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">Condition B</span>
        <span style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-size:19px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f">Breadth</span>
      </div>
      <p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0;max-width:74ch">At least half of certified Status&nbsp;A SKUs have lots at or below 60% of the current limit. This is the test that stops one dominant brand from triggering a ratchet the rest of the market cannot meet.</p>
    </div>
    <div style="padding:17px 19px;border-bottom:1px solid #f2ece7">
      <div style="display:flex;align-items:baseline;gap:14px;flex-wrap:wrap;margin-bottom:8px">
        <span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:11px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">New limit</span>
        <span style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-size:19px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f">Where it lands</span>
      </div>
      <p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0;max-width:74ch">The new limit is the greater of the actual 80th percentile of Status&nbsp;A results or 80% of the current limit. The second clause is a step cap: no certified brand faces more than a 20% reduction in a single ratchet event.</p>
    </div>
    <div style="padding:17px 19px">
      <div style="display:flex;align-items:baseline;gap:14px;flex-wrap:wrap;margin-bottom:8px">
        <span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:11px;color:#9f2727;white-space:nowrap">Transition</span>
        <span style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-size:19px;line-height:1.3;color:#2b1f1f">Time to comply</span>
      </div>
      <p style="font-size:14px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0;max-width:74ch">Twelve to eighteen months from announcement to enforcement, with existing Status&nbsp;A certifications grandfathered through the transition period.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <p style="font-size:11.5px;line-height:1.55;color:#a9a1a1;max-width:84ch">The ratchet uses the program&rsquo;s internal lot-testing data; the limit-setting at publication uses external survey data. The two run at different times, on different data sources, for different purposes. Full mechanics are specified in the Program Manual.</p>
</section>

<section id="not-waiting" style="margin-bottom:52px">
  <h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">Why the ratchet does not wait for regulators</h2>
  <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">Because the ratchet reads the program&rsquo;s own certified-lot data, a limit tightens as the certified market improves rather than when a government revises a maximum level. A government limit sets where a published value starts; the certified market decides how far below that anchor it ultimately settles. Over successive annual cycles a limit can fall well below the sovereign floor that first anchored it, without any regulator acting.</p>
  <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">The mechanism is analyte-agnostic. The Tier&nbsp;2 metals &mdash; nickel, tin, aluminium, and hexavalent chromium &mdash; are adopted directly from the government maximum at publication, but they ratchet on the same terms as the Tier&nbsp;1 toxics afterward, because every certified lot is tested against the full ten-analyte panel and the program therefore holds the data to tighten each one.</p>
  <p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">The program&rsquo;s role in the public regulatory process is to supply evidence &mdash; the synthesised literature behind each limit &mdash; not to author the numbers it later certifies against. Keeping those roles separate is what lets a published limit cite a government standard as an independent authority rather than as the program&rsquo;s own preference.</p>
</section>
