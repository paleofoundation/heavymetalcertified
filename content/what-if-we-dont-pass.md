The most important result is not a number. It is knowing what the number means and what it triggers before the sample is submitted.

A readiness finding, a formal certification decision, a borderline analytical result, a later surveillance event, and an exceedance of an applicable legal limit are not interchangeable. A controlled program distinguishes them rather than allowing the word “failure” to collapse every consequence into one feared event.

## The result-consequence matrix

| Situation | Certification consequence | Public consequence | Required next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Private Readiness identifies missing evidence | No certification decision has been made | No public applicant or failed-product listing | Complete the defined evidence gap, change the proposed scope, or stop. |
| Private Readiness indicates that intervention is likely | Product should not enter formal certification yet | No public certification status | Investigate supplier, ingredient, process, packaging, facility, or formulation contributors. |
| Formal result is clearly within the applicable decision rule | Product may continue through the certification review | No public record until a certificate is issued and the applicable visibility choice is made | Complete technical and governance review. |
| Result falls inside the measurement-uncertainty decision interval | No pass or fail is assigned from that result alone | No public result | Follow the published confirmatory pathway; ad hoc testing into compliance is not permitted. |
| Result is above an HMTc certification threshold but not above an applicable legal threshold | Product is not presently eligible for the affected certification status | No public failed-applicant registry | Confirm where required, investigate the source, correct the condition, and reassess. |
| Result may exceed an applicable legal or regulatory limit | Certification review stops or escalates under the Program Manual | Disclosure and market consequences are governed by law, regulation, contracts, and the facts | Activate the brand's quality, regulatory, and legal response; certification confidentiality does not delay it. |
| Certified-product data show an early warning or adverse trend | Status and surveillance consequences follow the published program rules | Public effect depends on the actual status and verification rules | Increase monitoring, investigate, and open CAPA where required. |
| A certified product has a confirmed nonconformity | Status, mark use, hold-and-release, CAPA, suspension, or revocation follow the governing edition | The public certificate record must not misstate the product's current status | Contain, correct, verify effectiveness, and satisfy reinstatement rules where available. |
| Concealment, manipulation, selective omission, or unauthorized retesting occurs | Data-integrity rules can trigger suspension or revocation | Public status follows the resulting certification determination and legal duties | Preserve records, escalate immediately, and follow the integrity and legal-response process. |

This matrix is an orientation layer. The governing Program Manual, standard edition, applicable law, executed agreement, and case facts control the actual decision.

## A certification miss is not automatically a legal violation

A certification standard, a retailer specification, an agency action level, and a legally binding maximum are different instruments. One result can implicate more than one, but the questions must be answered separately:

| Question | Decision-maker |
|---|---|
| Does the result meet the HMTc certification standard and decision rule? | Certification program under the governing edition. |
| Does the result comply with every applicable law in every relevant market? | Brand regulatory and legal functions, with competent counsel where needed. |
| Does the result violate a retailer, customer, supplier, insurer, or financing agreement? | The parties and the governing contract. |
| Does the result require a recall, report, market withdrawal, notice, or hold? | Applicable law, regulator expectations, product facts, and the brand's response process. |
| May the mark remain on new production or in marketing? | The HMTc status and mark-use rules. |

HMTc should never imply that its private process immunizes a brand from another obligation.

## Borderline results

The Program Manual uses a measurement-uncertainty decision rule. A result is not converted into a pass merely because its central estimate sits below a limit, and it is not converted into a fail merely because its central estimate sits above one when the uncertainty interval does not resolve the question.

A borderline outcome requires a predefined confirmatory action. Depending on the rule and matrix, this may include re-analysis of the same extract, a new sample from the same lot under strict chain of custody, or hold-and-release. The action and rationale must be documented. Unplanned repeat testing, laboratory shopping, undisclosed resampling, and selective reporting are not acceptable routes to a preferred answer.

## Before certification and after certification are different

The current Confidential Remediation Track is a pre-certification entry and support pathway for a product that is not yet certifiable but remains within applicable legal limits. It does not authorize the mark and does not preserve a certificate that has not yet been issued.

Once a product is certified, later nonconformities are governed by the status, surveillance, CAPA, mark-use, suspension, revocation, and reinstatement rules in the applicable Program Manual. The public site should not promise that voluntary disclosure leaves a certificate or mark unaffected unless a separately ratified post-certification protocol says so.

## What remains private

The program does not publish a list of Private Readiness participants or unsuccessful applicants. Confidential business information is handled under the applicable agreement and access controls.

This does not create attorney-client privilege or work-product protection, and it does not prevent legally required disclosure. A regulator request, legal process, reporting duty, recall, market correction, or contractual notice is not suspended by participation in HMTc.

## The useful promise

HMTc cannot promise that every product will pass. It can make the process more predictable by publishing the standard, analytical decision rules, consequence classes, confirmation routes, status effects, and next actions before the result arrives.
