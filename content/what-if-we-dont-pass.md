A readiness finding, a formal certification decision, a borderline analytical result, a later surveillance event, and an exceedance of an applicable legal limit are not interchangeable. Each carries its own consequence, and they are held apart here.

## The result-consequence matrix

| Situation | Certification consequence | Public consequence | Required next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Private Readiness identifies missing evidence | No certification decision has been made | No public applicant or failed-product listing | Complete the defined evidence gap, change the proposed scope, or stop. |
| Private Readiness indicates that intervention is likely | Product should not enter formal certification yet | No public certification status | Investigate supplier, ingredient, process, packaging, facility, or formulation contributors. |
| Formal result is clearly within the applicable decision rule | Product may continue through certification review | No public record until a certificate is issued and the visibility choice is made | Complete technical and governance review. |
| Result falls inside the measurement-uncertainty decision interval | No pass or fail is assigned from that result alone | No public result | Follow the published confirmatory pathway; testing into compliance is not permitted. |
| Result is above an HMTc threshold but not above an applicable legal threshold | Product is not presently eligible for the affected certification status | No public failed-applicant registry | Confirm where required, investigate the source, correct the condition, and reassess. |
| Result may exceed an applicable legal or regulatory limit | Certification review stops or escalates under the Program Manual | Disclosure and market consequences are governed by law, regulation, contracts, and the facts | Activate the brand's quality, regulatory, and legal response; certification confidentiality does not delay it. |
| Certified-product data show an early warning or adverse trend | Status and surveillance consequences follow the published program rules | Public effect depends on the actual status and verification rules | Increase monitoring, investigate, and open CAPA where required. |
| A certified product has a confirmed nonconformity | Status, mark use, hold-and-release, CAPA, suspension, or revocation follow the governing edition | The public certificate record must not misstate the product's current status | Contain, correct, verify effectiveness, and satisfy reinstatement rules where available. |
| Concealment, manipulation, selective omission, or unauthorized retesting occurs | Data-integrity rules trigger suspension or revocation | Public status follows the resulting determination and legal duties | Preserve records, escalate immediately, and follow the integrity and legal-response process. |

This matrix is an orientation layer. The governing Program Manual, standard edition, applicable law, executed agreement, and case facts control the actual decision.

## A certification miss is not automatically a legal violation

A certification standard, a retailer specification, an agency action level, and a legally binding maximum are different instruments. One result can implicate more than one, but the questions are answered separately:

| Question | Decision-maker |
|---|---|
| Does the result meet the HMTc standard and decision rule? | The certification program, under the governing edition. |
| Does the result comply with every applicable law in every relevant market? | The brand's regulatory and legal functions, with competent counsel where needed. |
| Does the result violate a retailer, customer, supplier, insurer, or financing agreement? | The parties and the governing contract. |
| Does the result require a recall, report, market withdrawal, notice, or hold? | Applicable law, regulator expectations, product facts, and the brand's response process. |
| May the mark remain on new production or in marketing? | The HMTc status and mark-use rules. |

The program never implies that its private process immunizes a brand from another obligation.

## Borderline results

The Program Manual applies a measurement-uncertainty decision rule (coverage factor k = 2, roughly 95% confidence). A lot passes only if the result plus its uncertainty is at or below the limit; it fails only if the result minus its uncertainty is above the limit; a result in between is borderline and is resolved by a predefined confirmatory action before any status is assigned.

Depending on the rule and the matrix, confirmation may include re-analysis of the same extract, a new sample from the same lot under strict chain of custody, or hold-and-release. The action and its rationale are documented. Unplanned repeat testing, laboratory shopping, undisclosed resampling, and selective reporting are not acceptable routes to a preferred answer.

## Before certification and after certification are different

The Confidential Remediation Track is a pre-certification entry and support pathway for a product that is not yet certifiable but remains within applicable legal limits. It does not authorize the mark, and it does not preserve a certificate that has not been issued.

Once a product is certified, later nonconformities are governed by the status, surveillance, CAPA, mark-use, suspension, revocation, and reinstatement rules in the applicable Program Manual edition.

## What remains private

The program does not publish a list of Private Readiness participants or unsuccessful applicants. Confidential business information is handled under the applicable agreement and access controls.

This does not create attorney–client privilege or work-product protection, and it does not prevent legally required disclosure. A regulator request, legal process, reporting duty, recall, market correction, or contractual notice is not suspended by participation in HMTc.

## The useful promise

HMTc cannot promise that every product will pass. What it can do is make the process predictable: the standard, the analytical decision rules, the consequence classes, the confirmation routes, the status effects, and the next actions are all published before the result arrives.
