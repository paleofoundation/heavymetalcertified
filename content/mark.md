A certification mark is not a general brand logo. It is a controlled, product-specific claim that must travel with enough scope for a reader to understand what was tested and which certificate supports it.

## What the mark asserts

| It does assert | It does not assert |
|---|---|
| The named product and form were evaluated under the standard and edition identified in the certificate. | That the product is “safe,” “heavy metal free,” free of risk, or free of every metal. |
| The required panel covered eight priority metals through ten analyte determinations and applicable reflex rules. | That analytes outside the panel were controlled. |
| The certification decision used qualified laboratory evidence, the required baseline, and ongoing surveillance rules. | That another product, flavor, form, size, facility, or brand is certified. |
| Public limits, product basis, status, and certificate scope can be checked. | Any claim about nutrition, sustainability, sourcing, purity, or another attribute. |

The mark states conformity to a published certification rule. It is not a health guarantee or an assurance of zero exposure.

The public files above are reference previews. They do not grant mark rights. A certified product receives a controlled production package tied to its certificate, approved scope, authorized variant, and artwork-review record.

## Reproduction specification

| Element | Required treatment |
|---|---|
| Primary color | Certification red `#9F2727` on white or light neutral backgrounds. |
| Reversed color | White `#FFFDFB` on certification red or a sufficiently dark, quiet background. |
| Aspect ratio | Preserve the supplied 1126:1314 artwork ratio. Never redraw or crop. |
| Clear space | Keep empty space on every side equal to one eighth of the mark’s displayed width. No copy, rules, package seams, or other marks may enter it. |
| Minimum print width | 18 mm for the mark. Increase it if production conditions make the internal lettering unclear. |
| Minimum digital width | 72 CSS pixels for a certification claim. The small navigation emblem on this website is site identity, not an on-product certification claim. |
| Background | Use a solid, high-contrast field. Do not place on photography, gradients, patterns, transparent packaging over variable contents, or colors that impair the outline or lettering. |
| Distortion | No rotation, skew, outline, shadow, glow, bevel, animation, recoloring, separation, or change in proportions. |

Final artwork must remain legible after the actual print process, material, varnish, shrink, emboss, and production tolerance are considered. Meeting a nominal dimension does not excuse an unreadable result.

## Required scope line

Where space permits, pair the mark with this controlled line:

> Tested against published limits for eight priority metals · ten-analyte panel · ongoing verification

The scope line must remain visually subordinate to the mark, set no smaller than 6 pt in print or 12 CSS pixels digitally, and appear close enough that a reader will understand the relationship. A shorter approved digital label may read “Ten-analyte testing · published limits · ongoing verification.”

Do not replace the scope line with “safe,” “non-toxic,” “free from heavy metals,” unqualified superiority language, or another claim broader than the certificate supports.

## QR code and verification pairing

When a QR code accompanies the mark, it must resolve directly to the product-specific verification record or a controlled redirect that preserves that destination.

| QR control | Rule |
|---|---|
| Destination | HTTPS product or certificate verification record; no generic homepage or campaign landing page. |
| Printed size | At least 20 mm square, subject to a successful scan test on final production material. |
| Digital size | At least 96 CSS pixels square. |
| Quiet zone | Preserve at least four QR modules on all sides. |
| Pairing | Place within one mark-width of the mark and label it “Verify product certification.” |
| Fallback | Print a human-readable certificate or product code. Do not make QR scanning the only route. |
| Approval | Verify the final destination, contrast, size, and scan performance before release. |

## Who may display it

| Product status or stage | Mark use |
|---|---|
| Status A — Certified, Full | Permitted within the approved product scope. |
| Status B — Certified, Transitional | Permitted only with the required transitional disclosure and current authorization. |
| Status C — Probation | Not permitted on new production. |
| Status D — Suspension | Not permitted. |
| Status E — Revocation | Not permitted. |
| Private Readiness or Confidential Remediation | Not permitted; these are pre-certification stages. |

Authorization is per product and form, not brand-wide. A certified powder does not authorize the mark on a ready-to-feed version; a certified flavor does not authorize every flavor; and a brand-level website claim must not imply portfolio-wide certification.

## Artwork review and change control

Before release, the licensee submits the actual package, ecommerce image, retailer tile, advertisement, or other use with the product code, certificate, market, dimensions, background, scope line, QR destination, and surrounding claims.

Approval applies only to the submitted use. Re-review is required when the product, form, formula, facility, mark size or color, scope line, QR destination, surrounding claims, package structure, market, or governing certificate changes materially.

Approval of artwork does not transfer responsibility for the brand’s complete labeling, advertising, legal, and retailer obligations.

## Claims discipline

Supportable language stays within the certificate record:

- “Heavy Metal Tested & Certified”
- “Tested against published limits for eight priority metals”
- “Ten-analyte testing under ongoing verification”
- The exact standard, edition, product, form, status, and certificate identifier

Prohibited language includes “heavy metal free,” “no toxic metals,” “100% safe,” “zero risk,” or any wording that implies the absence of metals, elimination of exposure, portfolio-wide certification, or control of an analyte outside the certificate scope.

## Suspension, removal, and re-entry

When mark authorization ends, the licensee must stop new production, digital use, advertising, retailer content, marketplace listings, sales materials, and any other new representation within the period stated in the notice. Existing inventory treatment follows the governing status, law, facts, and written direction; it is not assumed.

Re-entry requires three consecutive passing lot results at Status A or B levels and closure of the related CAPA before mark use resumes. A later authorization must be recorded; one favorable lot does not restore the mark.

## Public visibility and verification

A brand may control optional directory visibility where the governing manual permits it. That choice does not convert a public directory into an authoritative census and does not authorize an unverifiable mark.

A certificate or product code presented to a customer must resolve through the authoritative product-specific verification route. Absence from an optional browsable directory is not, by itself, proof of misuse; an unresolved, mismatched, expired, suspended, or revoked verification record is the relevant signal.
