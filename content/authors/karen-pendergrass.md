## Biography

Karen Pendergrass is a standards architect and microbiome researcher whose work connects contaminant evidence, food exposure, and microbial metallomics. She is the founder of the Institute of Contaminant Standards and the Paleo Foundation, and the founder of Microbiome Medicine and WikiBiome.

Her work spans evidence systems, certification standards, and public scientific reference libraries. At the Institute of Contaminant Standards, her roles include standards architecture and research leadership. The Heavy Metal Index maintains the literature reference; Heavy Metal Certified publishes the certification program and its standards.
