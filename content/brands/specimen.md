## Program-controlled product record

| Field | Illustrative value |
|---|---|
| Legal holder | Example Brand Company |
| Product | Example Stage 1 Formula |
| Form and basis | Powder, as placed on market |
| Certificate | EXAMPLE — not issued |
| Governing rule | Example standard and named edition |
| Panel | Eight priority metals / ten analyte determinations |
| Status | Example only — no live status |
| Effective period | Not applicable |
| Verification | Disabled until a real certificate exists |

In production, the program controls these facts and links them to the authoritative verification record. A directory page cannot broaden the scope, alter the status, omit a required transitional disclosure, or turn one product certificate into brand-wide certification.

## Product-specific scope

A real profile may contain more than one product, but each product retains a separate certificate, form, basis, governing edition, status, effective period, and mark authorization. Shared branding does not merge the records.

## Brand-supplied content

A participating brand may supply a product image, factual company description, and approved links. That material must be labeled as brand-supplied, remain visually separate from the conformity record, and stay within the authorized claim.

It may not imply safety, zero metals, certification of unlisted products, or control of analytes beyond the certificate scope.

## Press and retailer materials

Controlled mark artwork, permissions letters, certificates, machine-readable records, and approved boilerplate are issued through the product authorization. This public specimen does not distribute production artwork or create mark rights.
