<section id="quick" style="margin-bottom:52px"><h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">Quick read</h2><p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">Australia New Zealand Food Standards Code – Standard 1.4.1 – Contaminants and Natural Toxicants, and Schedule 19 – Maximum Levels of Contaminants and Natural Toxicants is a binding contaminant standard in force in Australia and New Zealand. It sets 5 infant and child food maximum levels across 3 of the eight HMTc metals — lead, total arsenic, and aluminium — transcribed here from Standard 1.4.1: FRLI F2015L00408 (current compilation F2022C00972, No.2); Schedule 19: FRLI F2015L00454 (current compilation F2024C00908, No.6, up to Amendment No. 231). HMTc records it because a certification standard adopts the strictest maximum level any credible government sets worldwide, and cites this instrument wherever it holds that value. This page records the law; it does not set or justify any HMTc threshold.</p></section>
