<section id="quick" style="margin-bottom:52px"><h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">Quick read</h2><p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">List of Contaminants and Other Adulterating Substances in Foods — Part 2: Maximum Levels (incorporated by reference into Division 15 of the Food and Drug Regulations, C.R.C., c. 870) is a binding contaminant standard in force in Canada. It sets 2 infant and child food maximum levels across 2 of the eight HMTc metals — lead and inorganic arsenic — transcribed here from Incorporated by reference under B.15.001, Division 15, Food and Drug Regulations (C.R.C., c. 870); list first established 2016-05-04 (SOR/2016-74); relevant modifications NOM/ADM C-2020-2 (Lead) and NOM/ADM C-2022-2 (inorganic Arsenic). HMTc records it because a certification standard adopts the strictest maximum level any credible government sets worldwide, and cites this instrument wherever it holds that value. This page records the law; it does not set or justify any HMTc threshold.</p></section>
