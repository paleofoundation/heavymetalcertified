<section id="quick" style="margin-bottom:52px"><h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">Quick read</h2><p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/915 of 25 April 2023 on maximum levels for certain contaminants in food and repealing Regulation (EC) No 1881/2006 is a binding contaminant standard in force in the European Union. It sets 25 infant and child food maximum levels across 6 of the eight HMTc metals — lead, inorganic arsenic, cadmium, mercury, nickel, and tin — transcribed here from (EU) 2023/915 (consolidated 02023R0915 — EN — 08.10.2025 — 006.001). HMTc records it because a certification standard adopts the strictest maximum level any credible government sets worldwide, and cites this instrument wherever it holds that value. This page records the law; it does not set or justify any HMTc threshold.</p></section>
<section id="instrument" style="margin-bottom:52px"><h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">Mandate &amp; scope</h2><p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">This page records the infant and child food heavy-metal maximum levels set by Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/915 of 25 April 2023 on maximum levels for certain contaminants in food and repealing Regulation (EC) No 1881/2006 ((EU) 2023/915 (consolidated 02023R0915 — EN — 08.10.2025 — 006.001)), the instrument of European Commission, 2023. Every value below is transcribed from the primary text and is the value an HMTc standard cites when this jurisdiction binds a certification limit. It is a record of law, not an HMTc threshold.</p><p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">It is the broadest infant and child food set tracked here and, alongside Brazil, the most complete: six of the eight HMTc metals carry a level, and it is the only tracked instrument to set an infant-relevant nickel level.</p><div style="border:1px solid #e8e1db;background:#fffdfb;padding:16px 18px;display:flex;align-items:center;justify-content:space-between;gap:18px;flex-wrap:wrap"><div style="min-width:0"><div style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-size:17px;line-height:1.35;color:#2b1f1f">(EU) 2023/915, official text</div><div style="font-size:12.5px;line-height:1.6;color:#a9a1a1;margin-top:3px">Published by European Commission; retrieved 2026-08-05.</div></div><a href="https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX:02023R0915-20251008" style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:10.5px;letter-spacing:.08em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#fff;background:#9f2727;border:1px solid #9f2727;border-radius:2px;padding:8px 15px;text-decoration:none;white-space:nowrap" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Primary source &nearr;</a></div></section>
