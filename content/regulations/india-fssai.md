<section id="quick" style="margin-bottom:52px"><h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">Quick read</h2><p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">Food Safety and Standards (Contaminants, Toxins and Residues) Regulations, 2011 — consolidated Compendium, Version IX is a binding contaminant standard in force in India. It sets 5 infant and child food maximum levels across 4 of the eight HMTc metals — lead, total arsenic, cadmium, and tin — transcribed here from FSS (Contaminants, Toxins and Residues) Regulations, 2011, regulation 2.1 (Metal Contaminants); consolidated Compendium version IX dated 03.02.2026. HMTc records it because a certification standard adopts the strictest maximum level any credible government sets worldwide, and cites this instrument wherever it holds that value. This page records the law; it does not set or justify any HMTc threshold.</p></section>
