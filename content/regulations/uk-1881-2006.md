<section id="hmtc" style="margin-bottom:44px"><h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">How this interacts with HMTc</h2><p style="font-size:15.5px;line-height:1.75;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0;max-width:84ch">HMTc certification standards adopt the <em>strictest existing</em> maximum level set by any credible government worldwide.
