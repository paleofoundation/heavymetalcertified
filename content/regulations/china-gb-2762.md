<section id="quick" style="margin-bottom:52px"><h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">Quick read</h2><p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">National Food Safety Standard — Maximum Levels of Contaminants in Foods is a binding contaminant standard in force in China. It sets 17 infant and child food maximum levels across 7 of the eight HMTc metals — lead, inorganic arsenic, total arsenic, cadmium, mercury, tin, and total chromium — transcribed here from GB 2762-2022. HMTc records it because a certification standard adopts the strictest maximum level any credible government sets worldwide, and cites this instrument wherever it holds that value. This page records the law; it does not set or justify any HMTc threshold.</p></section>
<section id="instrument" style="margin-bottom:52px"><h2 style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-weight:400;font-size:30px;line-height:1.2;color:#2b1f1f;border-bottom:1px solid #e8e1db;padding-bottom:10px;margin:0 0 20px">Mandate &amp; scope</h2><p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">This page records the infant and child food heavy-metal maximum levels set by National Food Safety Standard — Maximum Levels of Contaminants in Foods (GB 2762-2022), the instrument of National Health Commission (NHC) and State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR), 2022. Every value below is transcribed from the primary text and is the value an HMTc standard cites when this jurisdiction binds a certification limit. It is a record of law, not an HMTc threshold.</p><p style="font-size:16.5px;line-height:1.78;color:#3f3a3a;margin:0 0 18px;max-width:72ch">GB 2762 is unusual in tracking total arsenic and total chromium alongside the inorganic-arsenic and lead levels most bodies set, so it appears in comparisons where few others do.</p><div style="border:1px solid #e8e1db;background:#fffdfb;padding:16px 18px;display:flex;align-items:center;justify-content:space-between;gap:18px;flex-wrap:wrap"><div style="min-width:0"><div style="font-family:Lora,serif;font-size:17px;line-height:1.35;color:#2b1f1f">GB 2762-2022, official text</div><div style="font-size:12.5px;line-height:1.6;color:#a9a1a1;margin-top:3px">Published by National Health Commission (NHC) and State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR); retrieved 2022-06-30.</div></div><a href="https://gdgrain.gd.gov.cn/attachment/0/536/536928/4294867.pdf" style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:10.5px;letter-spacing:.08em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#fff;background:#9f2727;border:1px solid #9f2727;border-radius:2px;padding:8px 15px;text-decoration:none;white-space:nowrap" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Primary source &nearr;</a></div></section>
