<section id="how-it-works" style="margin-bottom:52px">
  <h2>How certification works</h2>
  <p>Certification attaches to a product, not to a brand as a whole. The application identifies the correct subcategory and native basis; testing then compares the product with every published value in that standard. Passing products receive a certificate whose scope and current status can be checked publicly.</p>
