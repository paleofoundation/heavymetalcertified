## How certification works

Certification attaches to a product, not to a brand as a whole. An application identifies the correct subcategory and native basis; testing then compares the product against every published value in that standard, across the full ten-analyte panel. A passing product receives a certificate whose scope and current status can be checked publicly, and the mark is withdrawn from a product later confirmed above its published limit. Begin from the governing row in the [standards register](/standards).

## Get certified

Certification is designed to create a plan, not a trap. A brand can read the applicable limit before it tests, and can enter a nonpublic readiness process before a product is ready to carry the mark. [For brands](/for-brands) sets out that controlled path; the [certification comparison](/compare) shows how the ten-analyte panel compares with narrower marks; [the readiness pathway](/program/readiness) explains how a product that is not yet ready reaches conformance; and [apply](/apply) opens the intake.

## Public register

Verification is a direct lookup. The certificate record, not a logo alone, states the covered product, the standard, the basis, the status, and the period. [Verify a certificate](/verify) by lot, certificate number, or product code; browse the [certified brands directory](/brands) of holders who elected public listing; and read the [scope of the mark](/mark) for exactly what it does and does not assert.

## For your team

Retailers setting an approved-certification list should start with [for retailers](/for-retailers); legal and quality teams evaluating the evidence with [for legal & QA](/for-legal-qa). Active certificate holders manage documents, lot records, and mark use in the [Brand Control Room](/portal). When a lot falls short, [corrective action](/program/corrective-action) defines the investigation and retest, without changing the published standard.
