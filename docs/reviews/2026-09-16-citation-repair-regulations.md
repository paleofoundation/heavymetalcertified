documents were recovered. No program threshold or standard dataset changed.

## Primary evidence

- [Thai FDA instrument page](https://food.fda.moph.go.th/food-law/announ-moph-414)
  links the [English PDF](https://food.fda.moph.go.th/media.php?id=509427551085666304&name=P414_E.pdf).
  Read clauses 1–7 and Annex 1. The supplement lead entry on PDF page 7 is
  1 mg/kg for the product as prepared for consumption. The translation explicitly
  yields to Thai text. No adult-body-weight derivation is stated there. The
  review identifies the 2020 instrument; it is not an exhaustive amendment audit.
- [FDA Pet Food](https://www.fda.gov/animal-veterinary/animal-foods-feeds/pet-food):
  read introduction, ingredient, labeling and inspection/compliance sections.
  General animal-food safeguards and the distinction between federal duties and
  state model-based labeling support the corrected legal context.
- [HMI homepage](https://heavymetalindex.com/): read navigation, displayed update
  date and library description. The homepage identifies an evolving reference
  library; it does not identify a frozen pet-food occurrence dataset.
- [Original EU regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/915/oj): reused
  the exact 15 September primary read of Articles 2–3 and the selected Annex
  cadmium entries retained in the pet-food unresolved record, with original-act
  identity/scope evidence retained in reviewed EU panels. The 16 September
  browser attempt encountered EUR-Lex's challenge; no new full-text access is
  claimed. The published explanation is limited to the cited original act.
- The public AAFCO model-regulation excerpt was read again. It contains
  omissions and does not justify a finding that the entire Official Publication
  or NRC materials contain no relevant limits. It was not mislabeled as a
  complete reviewed edition; the corrected reference names FDA instead.

## Related prose corrections

Reassessing complete pages also corrected contradictions already documented in
their retained primary-source reviews:

- Pet food: the pheasant lead comparison concerns human-market pheasant, and its
  product exceedance rates concern feed maxima; assayed metals are distinguished
  from detections; the Lebanon/UAE comparison is observational; human-average
  intake is not a toxicity threshold; the dog-snack experiment reported
  nondetection; the marine arsenic exception and general feed safeguards are
  included. Smaller body size alone no longer implies greater exposure.
- Supplements: removed universal adult-only assumptions, unverified USP
  numerical conversions, global minimum/uniqueness assertions and unsupported
  routine family-sharing claims. Preserved product-specific formula categories,
  powder/liquid differences, FSANZ preparation/transition limitations and EFSA's
  distinction between mean and high-percentile exposure. Corrected the
  Proposition 65 no-observable-effect criterion.

The page bodies retain the voluntary HMTc benchmark explanation and distinguish
source provenance from evidence of safety in a different product or population.
Research-process details are kept in this report and drawer provenance.
