## Evidence and editorial findings

- Mining studies require species, tissue, moisture basis and local setting.
  The Egyptian study has contradictory unit labels. River-survey percentages
  describe eligible river kilometers, not a fraction of individual fish.
  Codex supports source prevention and supplementary local consumption advice.
- Organ-meat studies do not establish one universal tissue ordering or a fixed
  recipe multiplier. The Bangladesh chicken study found kidney cadmium below
  muscle. Some historical legal comparisons differ from current EU limits.
  Reference 7 has no identifiable issuing body, instrument, date or link and
  remains unresolved; relevant EU context is separately identified in refs 1/5.
- Subsistence-exposure models are not measured individual overexposure rates.
  FDA advice explicitly addresses locally caught fish and acknowledges
  subsistence fishers; EFSA explicitly assesses high consumers. These sources
  contradict the article's blanket retail-only framing.
- Tin reference 1 now offers the DOI and public PubMed abstract as reader links.
  Its reviewed full-text provenance remains recorded; the old reader mirror had
  an expired TLS certificate. Other tin references survived the partial import.

An independent review checked schema, source identity, evidence and reader copy.
