# HMI / HMTc Governing Principles

Cited by `data/standards/readiness-manifest.json` as the ruling for
certification-readiness. This file is the cert-side mirror of the five
co-equal objectives that govern both the Heavy Metal Index and Heavy Metal
Certified. The five are co-equal: no one of them may be sacrificed to
advance another.

## The five co-equal objectives

1. **Drive down contamination.** The program exists to lower heavy-metal
   burden in products that reach people, not to decorate a label.
2. **Protect consumers.** Limits, testing, and the mark are instruments of
   consumer protection. A certificate that cannot be trusted does not
   protect anyone.
3. **Protect brands who enter.** A brand that enters in good faith, tests
   honestly, and stays inside the published rules is not to be set up to
   fail. Certification is offered only when the program has high
   confidence the brand can remain in the program.
4. **Remain legally defensible.** Every published number traces to a named
   sovereign government maximum or a disclosed read-across. Nothing is
   invented. Incomplete claim-surface cells are not cert-ready.
5. **Become the de facto standard.** The program is written to be the
   instrument the market, retailers, and regulators can actually use.
   Opacity, one-off exceptions, and un-reproducible numbers defeat that.

## Method lock (Method v2 / CLAUDE.md Part 19)

- Claim-surface cells are `gov_floor` or `disclosed read_across` only.
- Big-8 totals (Pb, tAs, Cd, tHg, Ni, Sn, Al, Cr) must be numeric for
  cert-ready. Species are pathway reflexes (rice → iAs, and so on) and
  never replace the parent: species ≤ parent.
- A blank, monitored, subsumed, or null claim-surface cell is HOLD, not
  cert-ready. Incomplete cells are not counted as certified.

## Certification gates added 2026-09-19

- Certify only if there is at least 90% confidence the brand can remain
  in the program.
- Lot-testing schedules are assessed from what the brand's laboratory
  actually states (panel, LOQ, MU, capability), not from a fantasy grid
  alone.
