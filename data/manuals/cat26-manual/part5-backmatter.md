# Part 5: Method and glossary

## 5.1 How every limit is set

The method is a disclosed read-across, not an invented ceiling and not an unbounded control.

Seven metals (lead, cadmium, total arsenic, total mercury, nickel, tin, and total chromium) are read across from the ICH Q3D elemental-impurity limits [1][2]. Injectable anabolic-androgenic steroids, injectable peptide hormones, and nasal or sublingual preparations take the parenteral concentrations. Raw powders and oral SARMs take the oral concentrations.

Aluminium is not an ICH Q3D analyte. It uses the FDA injectable limit (25) on the injectable and nasal standards [3]. It uses the EU drinking-water limit (200) on raw powders and oral SARMs [4].

Inorganic arsenic, methylmercury, and hexavalent chromium do not carry a separate numeric ceiling. Each is capped at its parent total and tested on the program retest schedule. The species never replaces the parent.

Every figure above is the value already published on the standard record. This manual does not add a ceiling. Occurrence data carry no compliance weight [5].

Certification is metal safety only. It does not certify efficacy, legality, or approval status.

## 5.2 What this manual does not do

This manual does not reprint the per-analyte ceiling table. The five live standard pages are the derivation of each cell, and Part 3 lists them with their house DOIs. A change to a cell is made on the standard, then reflected here at the next edition.

## 5.3 Glossary

percent of limit: A single analyte's measured value divided by its applicable numeric ceiling, as a percentage. Used only as an operational testing trigger (the 80% confirmatory trigger). It is not a certification ladder.

Certification status: The product-level lifecycle: Certified, Remediation, Suspension, Revocation. Certified means every numeric ceiling is at or below 100% of its published limit, and every species is at or below its parent total, under the measurement-uncertainty rule. The mark is permitted only then. Remediation is not certified and carries no mark. Suspension halts certification. Revocation is permanent. No band above the limit keeps the mark. All claim analytes are equal at the gate.

priority toxics: Plain-language name for lead, cadmium, inorganic arsenic, and mercury, used only for toxicological clarity. It is not a status ladder and does not change the certification gate.

CAPA: Corrective and preventive action. Windows scale with status.

read-across: A disclosed mapping from the named instrument on the standard record. For this category, the seven metals map from ICH Q3D, and aluminium maps from the FDA injectable limit or the EU drinking-water limit, by standard.

native basis: The form in which the product is sold. Every Category 26 standard is as sold.

metal safety only: The only attribute the mark speaks to. Not efficacy, not legality, and not approval status.
